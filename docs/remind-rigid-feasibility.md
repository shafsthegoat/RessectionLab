# ReMIND-001 rigid alignment feasibility: retained negative result

The single declared T1/T2 registration attempt produced a finite rigid transform, but its diagnostic mutual information **decreased from 0.698614 to 0.683110 nats**. Normalized mutual information decreased from **1.234330 to 1.227765**, measured on the same 169,524 common-coverage sample centers. The fitted proposal remains **unselected and unaccepted**. Identity was only the initial control; it has not been accepted as a cross-frame correspondence either.

The fixed T1 and moving T2 retain their different source frame identifiers. Their native qforms were used for an orthogonal registration grid only after independently confirming maximum qform/sform corner differences below 0.001 mm. Inputs were resampled to 3 mm native-axis grids, with separate per-image intensity scaling. No source segmentation or tumor location entered optimization.

The frozen attempt used CPU one thread, an Euler rigid transform, Mattes mutual information with 32 bins, seeded 20% sampling, two resolution levels, and at most 100 iterations per level. Optimization used checkerboard-parity-zero fixed-image support; the diagnostic compared parity-one centers under common before/after moving-image coverage. These centers are excluded from the optimizer mask, but normalization, smoothing and interpolation share image information, and neighboring voxels are correlated. This is a diagnostic comparator, not a strict independent clinical test set.

The attempt completed in **1.73 seconds supervised**, including **0.667 seconds fitting**, with approximately **680 MiB peak RSS**, under its declared 120-second/2-GiB limits. The optimizer reached the first-level iteration limit and the second-level minimum step after 44 iterations. The returned transform makes a roughly 1 mm translation with a small rotation. Final moving field-of-view coverage was **89.08%** of the coarse fixed grid; this measures scan coverage, not brain, functional or vascular coverage.

The saved transform’s LPS sampling direction was checked against the exported RAS matrix and inverse. Nine coarse native-plane panels were viewed; they do not establish improved anatomical alignment. There are no expert landmarks or target-registration-error measurements. Neither this metric nor visual inspection authorizes cortical access, clinical use, or automatic annotation transfer.

All originals remain unchanged. Declaration, frozen executable source, one attempt log, optimizer trace, both transform directions, before/after images, coverage, resource supervision and the negative-result QC receipt are under `artifacts/remind-rigid-feasibility-v1/`. The implementation is `scripts/assess_remind_rigid_feasibility.py`; three focused physical-coordinate tests passed. **No retry, alternate initialization, or tuning was performed.** This result does not invalidate the separately verified native DICOM conversion.
