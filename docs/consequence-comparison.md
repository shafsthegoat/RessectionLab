# Source-bound consequence comparison and checked STOP

The native workbench can now compare recorded programs without a scalar reward.
It retains separate source-removal, elapsed-time, mechanical, thermal and vascular
dimensions, and leaves unsupported consequences null. A policy can select a
unique paired-scenario Pareto continuation or fall back to checked cleanup with
residual. That fallback does not claim the resection task was completed.

This is a development comparison of authored programs on the same corridor,
not a new search/RL result or complete glioma resection. Patient mechanics,
functional consequences and clinical outcomes remain unvalidated.

## Inputs, consequences and uncertainty

The [frozen declaration](../manifests/experiments/consequence-comparison-v1.json)
binds the existing UCSF-PDGM-0007 continuum archive by SHA256, nominal scene and
source geometry, exact 15-action prefix, all three alternatives, parameter
worlds and both policies. The primary archive and original recordings are
unchanged. All derivatives retain the same DEVELOPMENT patient role.

The common prefix includes the authored exposure and retractor acquisition.
Each candidate then executes its whole suffix, including release/withdrawal.
The alternatives are the existing short hold, sustained hold and STOP programs.
No rejected action or discarded probe is installed as successful tissue work.

The three worlds retain identical anatomy and action programs, changing only
the continuum's assumed relaxation time by factors 0.5, 1 and 2. These are
engineering sensitivity scenarios, not measured patient uncertainty, probability
samples, confidence intervals or independent patients. No parameter is fitted
or chosen from these results.

The vector separates cumulative and incremental quantities:

- Additional target removal is counted from original source labels after the
  exact common prefix. It does not establish histological margins or infiltration.
- Non-target removal, elapsed time and mechanical exposure retain the whole
  exposure/setup/history. A small incremental cost cannot erase prior source removal.
- Continuum strain² and stress² exposure are grouped by source material.
  Their units do not give injury thresholds or neurological probabilities.
- Explicit heat models supply temperature/exposure; explicit vascular models
  supply wound volume and declared interface deficits. Absent models or source
  coverage stay unavailable rather than becoming zero. Supply/drainage exposure
  uses the maximum declared regional interface deficit; potentially overlapping
  territories are not summed into a supposed total patient deficit.
- Motor, language and accepted acquired-shift consequences remain separately
  unavailable for this task. A cortical/subcortical probe label does not supply
  a motor/language outcome.

For each pair of continuing programs, dominance requires no worse consequences
in **every objective in every matched parameter world**, and a strict advantage
somewhere. It does not average worlds, mix favorable interval endpoints, assign
probabilities or combine harms with tunable weights. Source counts, units and
objective directions are fixed by the vector schema.

## Executed result

Every program/world pair was executed twice from its canonically reconstructed
common prefix. All suffix states, receipts and final vectors matched exactly.
The nominal programs also match the retained complete snapshots. Nine pairs
took 338.8 seconds locally, including repeated execution and equality checks;
this is not a rollout-throughput benchmark.

| Program | Additional target | Residual target | Whole non-target | Brain strain² exposure range | Whole modeled time |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Short hold | 8 mm³ | 18,800 mm³ | 4,940 mm³ | 0.12222–0.12444 mm³·s | 19.283 s |
| Sustained hold | 8 mm³ | 18,800 mm³ | 4,940 mm³ | 0.13863–0.14086 mm³·s | 21.083 s |
| STOP cleanup | 0 mm³ | 18,808 mm³ | 4,940 mm³ | 0 mm³·s | 4.183 s |

The short hold dominates the sustained hold for the declared modeled objectives
in all three worlds. The large shared access cost and residual target remain
visible; this eight-cell continuation is not presented as a successful complete
resection or a validated access approach.

The source-count/mechanics research policy requires at least 8 mm³ additional
target and zero additional non-target removal. It selects the short hold.
A second policy additionally requires vascular supply, motor, language and
accepted acquired-shift evidence. Those four endpoints are unavailable, so it
selects the executed STOP branch and explicitly reports the resection task
**not met**. These policies illustrate research eligibility and uncertainty;
their thresholds do not establish clinical safety.

## Saved evidence and desktop behavior

The desktop shows both recorded decisions, the consequence ranges, missing
endpoints and full source costs. Its replay buttons use the existing native
branch/timeline controller; they do not synthesize new actions. Interactive
replay uses the nominal scene, while the other worlds remain recorded
sensitivity results. Editing a compared branch or its common prefix creates a
new branch, preserving the recorded comparison and its native snapshots.

The app pins the declaration hash in code, including source anatomy, exact
worlds, alternatives, prefix and policies. A resealed artifact cannot reduce
that experiment or promote its role/clinical claims. Validation also checks
nominal checkpoint content, source occupancy/residuals, model ledgers and
cleanup, reconstructs declared scenario scene/profile identities, and checks
metadata/program bindings before recomputing the decisions. No artifact-supplied
filesystem path is used to choose the trusted declaration.

The self-computable report seal proves neither counterfactual causality nor
real-world fidelity. Counterfactual causal evidence is the recorded repeated
execution; native branch opening still performs the normal canonical replay.
Numerical agreement never promotes device, patient-anatomy or clinical gates.

Independent review passed 40 paired-world product-order oracle cases and 41
adversarial controls. Source, scene, profile, prefix, residual, ledger and
checkpoint tampering, missing worlds/alternatives/policies, typed values,
unknown coercion and invalid STOP claims are covered. Resolved findings and
their original checker outputs are preserved.

The 139 targeted Python checks, 1137 desktop tests and desktop build pass.

## Interactive reassessment of the checked alternatives

In Rehearse → Advanced research workflows → Recorded consequence comparison,
**Reassess checked alternatives** edits a separate declared research task over
this one admitted comparison. Open the common checkpoint before reassessing.
The editor supports minimum additional target removal (mm³), a **cumulative**
non-target removal budget (mm³), and required vascular, motor, language or
acquired-shift evidence. Optional cumulative strain²/stress² caps stay under
advanced controls and are unvalidated research quantities. The fixed objectives
are additional target removal, cumulative non-target removal and modeled duration.

The shared backend comparator recomputes eligibility, per-world violations and
paired-world Pareto dominance from the checked vectors. It does not generate or
optimize routes, execute new action programs, fit models or train RL. Each
alternative shows additional and residual target, cumulative non-target removal,
modeled duration, required unavailable evidence and its actual exclusion reasons.
Ambiguous Pareto fronts remain ambiguous. Checked STOP retains its executable
release/withdrawal program and residual task status, including unmet requirements.

With minimum 8 mm³ and cumulative budget 4,940 mm³, both continuations are eligible
and short hold is the unique frontier. Raising the minimum to 9 mm³ excludes both.
Requiring motor evidence also excludes both because that consequence is Unknown;
STOP remains executable with the declared task **not met**. These are policy
reassessments of existing evidence, not new physical or physiological validation.

Each explicit reassessment appends a sealed
`resectionlab.surgical-consequence-user-analysis.v1` revision under
`consequenceAnalyses`, bound to source identity/geometry, the exact checkpoint,
native program snapshots/actions, declared worlds, vectors and frozen declaration.
All revisions are preserved; the bounded history rejects a 33rd revision. The
original experiment, policies and independent declaration checks remain unchanged.
Changing branch/cursor makes the saved analysis historical; returning to the
exact common checkpoint revalidates it. Source, checkpoint, program or scenario
changes reject stale bindings. Draft edits invalidate the displayed decision
until explicit reassessment and never issue commands by themselves.

Inspect buttons use verified nominal native replay. They move the viewing cursor
without editing a source program. Native save/reopen preserves all revisions,
edited policy and the reproduced decision; observed replay retains historical
evidence without granting reassessment authority.
