# HBE v5 twelve-row generated comparator

`scripts/mechanics_hbe_v5_comparison.py` checks the frozen HBE_01_03 twelve-row mesh/step comparison on **generated compact stream readouts**. Call `compare_generated_rows(repository_root, rows)` with exactly the run IDs in `hbe-01-03-branch-calibration-v5.json`. It does not launch FEBio, read measured force/torque CSVs, fit material, or admit saved native output. The result always has `numerical_qualification_passed=false`, `native_execution_admitted=false`, `adapted_deck_authenticated=false`, `physical_validation_pass=null`, and `calibration_released=false`, even when the generated diagnostic screen passes. Its per-row input must explicitly say `generated_fixture_only=true` and `native_output_observed=false`; a native-origin claim is rejected.

The comparator checks the v5 run identity, full-versus-reconstructed representation, 61/121 frame shapes, exact signed input-coordinate schedule, finite signed force and all 75 three-component physical-probe displacements, the source-deck **hash declared by the frozen non-executable binding map**, and consistency of each generated stream's numerical flags. It does not open source or adapted deck bytes, so the reported source-byte and adapted-deck authentication fields remain false. The fixed v4 coordinate-only lists are used through `strict_coverage` to check all 30 compression and 30 tension coordinates against the complete solved interval with zero extrapolation. No response values are opened.

It retains signed differences at every one of the 61 shared states for all adjacent S60 meshes and the two S60/S120 comparisons. Four frozen v4 pair diagnostics are independently reproduced: compression N32→N36, compression N36 S60→S120, tension N16→N24, and tension N24 S60→S120. Their force limits are `1.6e-7 N + 2% max|fine force|` for mesh and `1.6e-8 N + 0.2% max|fine even-state force|` for step; maximum probe-vector limits are 8 and 0.8 µm. Odd S120 states never enlarge a common-state force denominator.

The original three-level mesh metrics are reported for four compression triplets and both tension co-primary triplets. The original N36 compression candidate gates only N24/N32/N36 **reaction and motion allowances** plus the latest conditional screen; its raw strict trend and older compression groups remain visible diagnostics, not extra gates. Tension requires both original co-primary triplet metrics, including strict reaction/motion trends above their floors. Compression records all four unequal-spacing force-order histories, latest-limit disagreement and two-latest-limit envelope at all 60 nonrest states. Only the two latest triplets must resolve each state; older null eligibility reasons remain visible. Replacing N36/S60 by N36/S120 **even** states must preserve compression state and group classifications. Replacing N24/S60 by N24/S120 even states must preserve both tension group classifications. The old negative N32 envelope result is checked through its existing SHA256-bound declaration and retained separately as historical evidence; it is not substituted for a new v5 run.

Every adjacent mesh difference is also retained, with a separate list of exceedances that are **not** frozen pairwise gates. For example, a generated tension N8 sign reversal can exceed the N8→N12 pair limit while both co-primary trend groups pass. The comparator reports that limitation rather than inventing a sign or coarse-adjacent acceptance rule. A passing generated screen is never a force-fidelity or physical-validation result.

This is a source-only diagnostic slice. A later, separately reviewed native-admission layer must bind each supervised execution receipt and independently replay the raw node, element and solver files before a numerical specimen claim is possible. Even a successful twelve-row numerical study would still need separate measured-force calibration and withheld same-specimen torsion evaluation. Neither validates patient retraction, cutting, neurological injury or clinical route decisions.
