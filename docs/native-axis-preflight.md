# Native axis action-model preflight

The declaration is [`native-axis-preflight-v1.json`](../manifests/experiments/native-axis-preflight-v1.json). This measures resource costs before choosing a training budget. It does not train, select a policy checkpoint, or compare learning methods. Public execution remains gated by the orchestrator's release of tested, committed source.

The new RAW-only axis adapter uses the prior UCSF-PDGM-0004 source cells, native tools, hypothetical access, reward and deterministic world manifests. Its action/model hashes are distinct from the completed fixed-candidate studies. Defaults are thirteen declared columns, twenty-six primary rays, STOP, and at most three cuts. A fallback is tried only after a rejected primary. The full ledger is completed before any action is chosen.

Five episodes must finish: one nominal greedy episode on the first optimization seed, two replays of its frozen action sequence on the selection seeds, and two deterministic rollouts of an unchanged RAW seed-11 policy with fifteen action features, six state features and hidden width sixteen. The policy is frozen before either rollout; no optimizer, backward pass or gradient update exists in this workflow. Constructor seed zero prepares only the initial inventory and performs no transitions. Final and stress worlds remain unopened.

The worker has a 600-second cooperative total wall cap and a 6 GiB process peak-RSS cap. The launcher requests termination after 600 seconds from subprocess start and kills after a further 15 seconds if needed. RSS is checked at cooperative boundaries, not reserved or enforced by an OS allocation limit. Imports, geometry operations and receipt writes may cross a cooperative boundary; observed elapsed time and any cancellation are retained. A launcher timeout makes the result ineligible even if the worker subsequently exits successfully.

Costs are retained separately for case/native preparation, cold adapter construction, complete inventory inspection, one initial integrity-checked clone, resets, decisions, transitions including the next inventory, policy import/initialization/export, independent checks, and the full worker/launcher calls. Integrity, preview and cancellation counters are nested measurements within these phases; do not add them again to the total. Adapter counters restart on reset; the full reset timer also includes verification of the preceding state. The panels reuse one simulator, so their timings exclude the repeated clone/factory calls that the current generic learner performs. The clone probe supplies one measured cost, not an exact prediction of future update cost.

All five complete native histories receive an independent source-cell, frontier and complete-tool certificate. A certificate is reused only for identical normalized history, source and model hashes. The cancellation probe measures a request before cached inventory access; it is not a measurement of worst-case interruption inside a preview. Tiny tests cover interruption after a committed cut and preserve that cut and reward. Generic learner resumption after such an interruption remains outside this preflight.

`candidate-records.json` is diagnostic payload, not publication authority. Both final `launcher-status.json` and `profile/status.json` must be completed; the latter must report two eligible records and match the payload's SHA-256. Incomplete panels, source mutation, failed exports, failed geometry or timeouts retain their raw records with zero eligibility. Each attempt requires a new output directory. No automatic retries or revised settings follow an outcome.

Without `--execute`, [`preflight_native_axis.py`](../scripts/preflight_native_axis.py) writes only the declaration. With an execution release, supply a fresh output and the declared bundle explicitly. The launcher preserves exact source bytes and dependency versions, runs the worker from that copy, and retains process logs and preparation failures. Normal desktop and OS background activity remains uncontrolled.

The existing anatomy is still an unreviewed structural mirror derivative with hypothetical access. Motor/language evidence is absent; functional outcomes and clinical probabilities are unassessed. Two zero-perturbation selection worlds are deterministic replays, not uncertainty intervals or independent patients.
