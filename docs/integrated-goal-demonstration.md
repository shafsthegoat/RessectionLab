# Integrated rehearsal development demonstration

## Current cumulative re-entry milestone

**Staged source strategies → Resection sequence → Planned external re-entry**
extends the existing deterministic skill controller with complete external
entries, checked withdrawal between stages, and tool configuration changes only
outside tissue. It proposes a finite source-contact sequence from sealed routes
already in the current development case. Native motion, contact and exit checks
admit each stage; source contact alone does not establish feasibility. The
original actual access baseline remains fixed across every later entry.

The frozen two-patient native pilot completed five re-entry stages with three
tip sizes on UCSF-PDGM-0007: **172 mm³ target / 187 mm³ non-target** over the entire
approach, including initial access of 14/167. It completed two stages on 0005:
**25/171 mm³**, including initial access of 6/158. Independent direct native
replay verified both full trajectories, cleanup, original access accounting,
checkpoint recovery and unchanged source records. The 0007 endpoint improves
the removal pair over the earlier precision result of 137/197; it does not
dominate the lower-damage joint result of 104/114. The 0005 result remains below
the retained 33/174 original partial baseline. These are different bounded
searches, not a matched-compute controller superiority experiment.

Both larger task gates remain **unpassed**. The 0007 endpoint removes only
0.89% of enhancing target, reaches one declared spatial zone, and fails the
non-target/target ratio and meaningful configuration-contribution gates.
Neither case demonstrates a complete operation. Six-face exterior search is
also available as an opt-in for local staged work, within the original fixed
surface/pair budgets; its tested alternatives did not replace the stronger
saved baselines.

Joint sector task results remain 2/3 on 0007 and 0/3 on 0005; the frozen 0008
negative assessment remains 0/14 and is not training data. PPO has demonstrated
no incremental benefit over BC/search. The useful next step is more distributed
native clearance and target coverage within the original tissue budgets,
before scaling learned execution. Missing skull, complete vascular anatomy,
function and patient-calibrated outcomes remain unavailable.

The desktop also exposes **Staged source strategies** from an intact, declared
development access/sector comparison. This deterministic controller extends the
existing engine with short source-anchored pivots, axial work, checked historical
exit recovery, external tool reconfiguration and sealed native checkpoints. It
admits work only after checking its withdrawal, preserves rejected probes, and
counts previews, commits, recovery and replay against one bounded budget.
Native shaft refusals can propose a shorter stroke from the observed accepted
pose; that command must pass fresh native checks before a dwell at its actual
endpoint. Legacy CUSA withdrawal can use reconstructed integration-step paths.
Bulky staged evidence uses the existing bounded lossless archive codec while
native branches remain readable by the historical frozen-policy runtime.
`scripts/compare_staged_anatomy_strategies.py` provides the same native exporter.
The larger task requires substantial whole-target, spatial-zone and configuration
contributions; its 240-action gate is displayed separately from the original
48-action sector task. Checkpoint replay verification does not imply an
interactive controller-resume feature. Recorded playback remains distinct from
new planning or frozen inference. This extension has not replaced the stronger
saved sector baseline or established larger-task success. Local development
traces and independent replay/hidden-desktop results remain under `testcases/`.

The current [sector-strategy workflow](sector-strategies.md) adds actual
near/far target order, left/right CUSA arrangements, recorded model consequences
and three-seed hierarchical BC/PPO execution. The v6 stroke-policy demonstration
below remains historical source-bound evidence; use its frozen package or a
new compatible fit for current-checkout stroke inference. Local current results
and checkpoint paths are recorded in ignored `testcases/`.

The October 26 integration now runs real source import, anatomy inspection,
three geometry-dependent access simulations, frozen learned CUSA continuation,

not a complete operation or clinical validation. The full research goal remains

## Actual learned execution on the fresh source

The continuation task begins at a verified recorded access boundary before its
cleanup, using the original native engine state. No exposure is fabricated.
Its predeclared gate is at least 30 mm³ additional target, at least two admitted
non-STOP actor actions, at most 20 mm³ additional non-target removal, and checked
deactivation/withdrawal. The actor receives nine tool-frame options with 22
features from published current observations and declared task budgets. Source
arrays, previews and future outcomes are excluded from its inputs.

```sh
.venv/bin/python scripts/run_patient_cusa_learning.py \
  --source testcases/outputs/integrated-goal-20261026/fresh-patients/UCSF-PDGM-0007/access-comparison-v4/access-comparison.ressectionlab \
  --branch access-superior-access --source-role DEVELOPMENT \
  --output testcases/outputs/integrated-goal-20261026/my-cusa-run
```

The runner first measures the scripted comparator, fits behavior cloning and
tests its frozen live execution. It then performs two on-policy PPO collection
and update rounds, each with six sampled decisions and two optimizer epochs.
Actor-specific before/after hashes prove the actor changed independently of the
critic. The final checkpoint is cold loaded, makes its own observed-state
choices, and its effects are independently replayed. Both BC and PPO native
branches are exported separately with checkpoint, producer implementation,
runtime, physics-profile and fitting-receipt identities.

On fresh 0007, BC and PPO each selected `inward-2`, `inward-2`, STOP: **36 mm³
additional target, 0 mm³ additional non-target**, two admitted strokes and
successful cleanup. The current v6 run used 151 of its declared 200 physical transition
calls, 6.86 seconds wall and 606 MB peak process RSS. The bound includes source
and independent replay plus export verification. It is not GPU presentation
FPS. The initial untrained comparator also passed with a different endpoint;
no superiority over untrained, BC, script or search is established.

## Inspect and play in Electron

Open the combined learned case:

```sh

  "$PWD/testcases/outputs/integrated-goal-20261026/fresh-patients/UCSF-PDGM-0007/cusa-continuation-trained-v6/learned-continuations.ressectionlab"
```

In **Rehearse**, **Research Planning** compares the three real endpoints and
opens their branches. **Learned CUSA continuation after selected access**
distinguishes BC from PPO, shows additional modeled removal and checkpoint
identities, and opens the learned starting boundary. **Play replay** animates
actual authoritative instrument poses and anatomy changes; it performs no new
inference. The runner above is the real training and policy execution path.
Timeline scrubbing, pause, source-preserving save and reopen use the existing
shared native engine infrastructure.

To execute a policy anew, open either current access comparison, use
**Run a learned continuation**, choose the approach, click **Run frozen CUSA
checkpoint**, and select the compatible v6 `ppo-policy.pt`. The app snapshots
the current workspace, runs actual frozen observed-state inference without
optimizer updates, verifies the trajectory, and opens the saved outcome.
The input file is preserved. **Cancel frozen policy execution** stops the run;
failed display admission restores the input snapshot. Output evidence is under
`testcases/outputs/cusa-desktop/`. Completed cleanup does not promote a failed
task. The action currently requires the local checkout and matching runtime.

The real Electron verifier accepts `access`, `learned` (prepared rescue),
