# RHUH: prospective exploratory baseline

This [fixed declaration](../manifests/experiments/rhuh-preoperative-baseline-v1.json) precedes fitting, **not outcome inspection**: all 40 RHUH records were already examined for QA. No model has been fitted for this protocol. It studies observational association with recorded postoperative status; it does not estimate surgery-caused injury, unperformed-route risk, or a planner reward.

Use the [audited public CSV](rhuh-outcome-audit.md), SHA-256 `32d638906d34aaf8f66f5ec41c53c044216aed73bac22c776fb399bf2f741728`. Include RHUH-0001–0040 without exclusions. Preserve `No` (26), `Transient` (6), `Minor Persistent` (6), and `Major Persistent` (2); the sole endpoint maps the three deficit categories to 1 and `No` to 0. Missing never means `No`. All visits remain grouped by patient.

Compare exactly three methods: training-fold prevalence; fixed-L2 logistic regression using `Preoperative KPS`; and the same regression adding `Preoperative  contrast enhancing tumor volume (cm3)` (the source header has two spaces). Consume only the byte-bound adapter's preoperative projection. Identity and provenance are not predictors; exclude postoperative KPS, residual/EOR, treatment, survival and unverified presurgical pathology/molecular information.

For each sorted patient ID, train on the other 39 and predict that patient once. Standardize each model's predictors using only its training fold: population variance, constant-column scale 1. Logistic settings are C=1, L2, unpenalized intercept, LBFGS, tolerance 1e−8 and at most 1,000 iterations. Use untransformed KPS points and volume in cm³ before scaling. Numeric KPS assumes a linear log-odds term, not a linear harm scale. No tuning, weights, oversampling, imputation or calibration. Classify probabilities ≥0.5 as positive; no final full-cohort model.

Primary output is paired mean Brier difference, volume-plus-KPS minus KPS; negative favors the added predictor. Also report both models against prevalence, all patient probabilities/squared errors, logistic ROC-AUC and fixed-threshold confusions with sensitivities/specificities. Undefined ratios are null with reasons. Prevalence ROC-AUC is null: its leave-one-out probabilities inversely encode the held-out label through training prevalence. Its Brier score and confusion remain valid. Do not treat overlapping folds as independent observations or manufacture confidence intervals.

Run 99 fixed-seed label permutations, repeating every fold's scaling and fits. The diagnostic statistic is volume-plus-KPS Brier minus prevalence Brier; Monte Carlo p=(1+number of permuted statistics ≤observed)/100. This tests an exchangeable-label chance diagnostic, **not** whether volume adds information conditional on KPS. It cannot select a model. Separately delete each of the 14 positive patients, rerun 39-patient leave-one-out, and report paired-Brier changes, range and sign counts. These are influence diagnostics, not confidence intervals or reasons to exclude patients.

Before fitting, rotate every prohibited CSV column across patients while preserving identity, target and permitted predictors. Explicitly hash this derived shadow input and require identical projections. During observed validation, each fitted model must produce exactly the same shadow-input predictions; this adds no fits or patients.

Bound the entire worker to 180 seconds, one CPU thread and 1 GiB RSS, with 15 seconds termination grace and at most 9,092 logistic fits. No retries. Save source/runtime hashes, ordered folds, coefficients/scalers, convergence, phase costs and every failure. Nonfinite or unconverged fits invalidate the affected fold; incomplete observed predictions cannot produce complete metrics. Incomplete permutations/influence analyses retain planned denominators and null summaries.

Exact clinical assessment times, deficit domains, baseline focal deficits, severity/persistence definitions and feature-availability timestamps are unknown. Early postoperative imaging does not date clinical labels. Selection for extensive resection, treatment and recurrence imaging limits transportability. Two major cases cannot validate major-deficit risk. Root review, immutable source freeze and explicit execution release remain required.
