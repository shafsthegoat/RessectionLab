# Independent affine readout for the numerical cube

Status: **source-only software check; no n5 native run**. `scripts/mechanics_nonpatient_sparse_affine_readout.py` consumes the complete initial plus four load states of the declared `n5_affine` node and element streams. It uses the saved-output parser's exact record grammar and rejects missing, reordered, nonfinite, or unsynchronized fields. The direct parsed-record entry point exists for testing and expects output from that parser; it does not itself establish file origin. Existing validators read pinned local declaration/runtime manifests, but this readout does not open saved output or patient files. No FEBio process, patient anatomy, fitted tissue property, or physical measurement is involved.

At each state, the readout reconstructs the deformation gradient from **actual nodal positions** and the pinned tet10 shape derivatives at eight Gauss points. It evaluates the declared idealized Ogden numerical gauge there, integrates energy density over reference volume in SI units (Pa × m³ = J), and compares actual displacement, deformation Jacobian, and Cauchy stress against the independent homogeneous affine oracle. It also compares all saved element stress components, `J`, and strain-energy density against the reconstructed fields. The saved `sed` field is an arithmetic integration-point average, so its reference-volume integral is valid as an independent total-energy cross-check for this homogeneous affine case. It must not be generalized to a nonuniform field without revisiting that quadrature relation.

The signed boundary work is reconstructed solely from saved nodal reactions and displacements, `Σ R·u` in N·m = J, then compared with the oracle's negative `P:(F−I)V` convention for body-on-constraint reactions. Net force, current-coordinate moment, and reactions on free nodes are checked separately. A displacement/position inconsistency, wrong sign, zero reaction, or altered energy density cannot be rescued by another passing metric. Every state must pass. A maximum actual-gradient deviation is reported in dimensionless units but is not given a newly invented gate; full-node affine displacement, reconstructed stress, and `J` use the frozen declaration's limits. The per-element density gate is the frozen total-energy allowance divided by the declared 10 mm cube volume: `1e-8 J / 1e-6 m³ = 0.01 Pa`. This is a derived expression of the declared energy gate for the homogeneous fixture, not a tissue-fidelity tolerance.

Passing this readout means only that **supplied arrays** satisfy the frozen affine software oracle and signed work identity. It does not establish a native solve, exact executable/backend/source/output binding, reported solver residuals, convergence at n9/n13, displacement or force fit to tissue, or surgical usefulness. Native release must combine this with the separately reviewed saved-output checks, runtime/provenance and resource witnesses, then stop at the first failed case. The force/energy identities share the declared constitutive assumption; they cannot validate that assumption against tissue.

`tests/test_mechanics_nonpatient_sparse_affine_readout.py` builds explicitly synthetic exact affine fields and corner-integrated analytical tractions. Both parsed arrays and text round-trip pass all five states. Adversarial controls change interior deformation, saved stress, `J`, energy density, reaction sign, complete-state count, time, row count, and text validity; all fail the applicable gate. These fixtures are software controls, not simulator output or observations.
