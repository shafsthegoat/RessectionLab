# ReMIND-001: acquired native anatomy, development only

On October 4, 2026, the official [TCIA ReMIND release](https://www.cancerimagingarchive.net/collection/remind/)
and [IDC-supported metadata interface](https://github.com/ImagingDataCommons/idc-index)
were used to select the lowest eligible case number before accessing image pixels.
The official clinical workbook identifies case 001 as Astrocytoma. Eligibility used
histopathology alone; postoperative outcomes, molecular fields, image quality and
planner performance did not determine selection. Histopathology availability at the
planning time is unknown and is excluded from planner inputs and rewards.

`ReMIND:001` is one human development patient, assigned to annotation-assisted
geometry integration. It is neither an external evaluation patient nor evidence
of population pretraining. Existing BTC assignments and unopened UPenn images are
unchanged. No simulated anatomy or training examples were generated by this slice.

## Executed acquisition

The manifest `manifests/experiments/remind-001-structural-development-v1.json`
pins IDC v24 / idc-index-data 24.2.2 identities and 386 object URLs, lengths and
single-part S3 MD5 ETags before the first image request. Four series comprise
59,163,552 bytes: preoperative T1 postcontrast, T2 SPACE, cerebrum SEG and tumor
SEG. They were downloaded anonymously in 16.13 seconds; all expected lengths and
MD5s matched, and local SHA256 hashes were recorded. No intraoperative,
postoperative or collection-wide imaging was downloaded. UTC acquisition times
are receipt times, not evidence that metadata were available before surgery.

The client environment is isolated under ignored `build/idc-acquisition-venv`;
the application environment was not changed. Acquisition uses only exact public
IDC object URLs, rejects redirects, binds the HTTP request with If-Match, checks
response identity/length/ETag before publication, and writes a durable attempt
receipt. The original executed source is archived. A subsequent CLI guard also
rejects any changed prospective manifest. Twenty-one focused transport/coordinate
controls passed, including bad bodies, redirects, physical-axis ordering and
nonuniform planes. These are software controls, not additional training patients.

## Native conversion and remaining gates

Local immutable source DICOMs are under `data/remind_source/idc-v24/ReMIND-001/`.
Converted volumes and `native-conversion.json` are under
`data/remind_source/converted-native/ReMIND-001/`. Conversion preserves physical
LPS-to-RAS coordinates and pixel values without cross-sequence resampling.

| Volume | XYZ shape | Source label |
| --- | --- | --- |
| T1 postcontrast | 216 × 256 × 192 | structural MRI |
| T2 SPACE | 256 × 256 × 192 | structural MRI |
| Cerebrum | 459 × 576 × 357 | automatic BrainLab; 1,056,818.013 mm³ |
| Tumor | 61 × 63 × 59 | manual Glioma; 27,278.448 mm³ |

The initial endpoint-only slice affine exceeded the 0.001 mm position tolerance
because of decimal coordinate rounding. Fitting all slices reduced the maximum
residual to 0.000777 mm, retaining the threshold. The failed attempt and source
are preserved. All exported samples roundtrip exactly; the largest NIfTI affine
serialization corner error is below 0.000013 mm.

Each SEG shares the frame UID of the MRI named in its release description, but
the released objects lack explicit referenced-source SOP links. This limitation
is retained. The T1 and T2 frame UIDs differ; joint use requires independently
reviewed, measured registration. The cropped SEG extent is retained; space outside
it is not certified as assessed zero. Source labels are unreviewed research
annotations. Cortical access is unapproved, and functional, directional diffusion
and vascular coverage remain absent. `planner_case_ready` therefore remains false.
