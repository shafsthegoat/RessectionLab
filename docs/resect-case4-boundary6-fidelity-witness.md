# Case4 boundary6 saved-geometry fidelity witness (preparation only)

The [frozen declaration](../manifests/experiments/resect-case4-boundary6-fidelity-witness-v1.json) and [source](../scripts/mechanics_patient_mesh_boundary6_witness.py) prepare one possible read-only diagnostic of the **rejected** Case4 v4 mesh. They are not an execution authorization. No v4 source surface, mesh array, image, landmark, or during-US payload was opened in this preparation. An independently reviewed, exact committed-source archive and separate root release are required before a single local attempt may read the saved geometry. The output must stay in a private ignored directory.

The original v4 attempt returned 19,070 nodes and 10,512 tet10 elements but failed the unchanged **2 mm bidirectional full-surface gate**: source→mesh sampled maximum/upper bound 3.869/4.576 mm; mesh→source 2.264/3.254 mm. This witness can only localize discrepancies relative to the retained estimated T1 envelope. It cannot accept the mesh, diagnose a Gmsh seam or causal mechanism, establish anatomical suitability, simulate retraction/cutting, measure force or injury, or validate a clinical route. Case4 remains retrospective DEVELOPMENT because its comparison V outcomes were already exposed. A separate source-suitability audit currently blocks another Gmsh or FEM attempt.

The worker binds the rejected candidate receipt, complete four-array diagnostic manifest and files, original native surface and its array digests, v4 release/summary, and exact source blobs. It rejects altered identities, shapes/dtypes, non-finite geometry, invalid connectivity or an existing output. It derives the exterior from tet10 **corners** using the pinned `FACES` order and verifies 2,428 exterior vertices, 4,856 faces and 7,284 distinct straight midsides. No mesh generation or solver module is imported. B/V, before/during US, mask, MRI and policy weights are outside the allowed input set.

The worker replays the original grouped 1 mm barycentric lattice with 256-triangle chunks in both directions. It first requires the same sample counts, maxima and original cover-inclusive bounds within 1e-12 m. Each face's reported upper contribution is its sampled maximum plus **its original chunk's** cover radius. The immutable 2 mm gate remains failed. It records whole-face exceedance-area fractions, area-weighted face-max quantiles, edge-connected components, original lattice witness ordinals with independently checked nearest triangles, and corner/midside/face-centroid mesh→source probes. Normal changes are adjacent-face proxies, not curvature or patch IDs.

The prospective localization test uses 30 mm half-open cubes on a 5 mm absolute RAS grid. Each entire face with a sampled maximum >2 mm contributes its whole area to every cube containing its centroid. Candidate cubes must contain at least one witness-face centroid from either direction. Choose the candidate that maximizes the smaller of the two captured exceedance-area fractions; exact ties use the lexicographically smallest origin. Call the error **concentrated under this diagnostic** only when exceedance-face area is ≤5% of total surface area in *each* direction and one chosen cube captures ≥80% of exceedance-face area in *each* direction. Otherwise, the local-refinement premise is falsified at this scale. Failed hash or numerical replay is an **invalid experiment**, never evidence of diffuse error. A positive localization would motivate only a separately frozen hypothesis, not another mesh without source anatomy and sparse-solver feasibility gates.

One run is capped at 120 s outer/110 s cooperative, 2 GiB sampled group RSS, one numerical thread, 1.25 million distance queries, 16 MiB total/8 MiB per file, 32 files, zero retries. The expected exact count is 1,193,462 queries: 1,178,874 original lattice queries, 14,568 probes and 20 independent locator checks. Private outputs are `report.json`, `per-triangle-diagnostics.npz` and `acceptance.json` plus bounded supervision receipts. The report carries witness coordinates only inside the ignored private directory. A later independent saved-output audit must precede any aggregate publication. No raw geometry or witness coordinates belong in Git.

The worker requires a one-shot token generated by the supervised parent and bound to the release hash and attempt directory. A direct worker command without that launch context stops before saved-input access. Once the parent creates the attempt directory, it writes a terminal failure acceptance even if setup or supervision fails. The token hash, never the token itself, is retained in the private launch context; this prevents accidental bypass of the resource supervisor, not deliberate local code modification.
