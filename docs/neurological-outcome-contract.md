# Neurological outcome representation: source-first contract

This contract follows the neurological-harm charter and source audits. Its isolated RHUH ingestion implementation is described below; no outcome model or planning reward is implemented here. No patient images, protected BTC outcome rows or models were opened or run. Observed postoperative status, change from baseline, imaging injury and simulated exposure remain different endpoints.

RHUH's 40 records have no blank/null-like cells, but structurally absent fields remain unknown. Early postoperative MRI within 72 hours does **not** date clinical labels. [Source audit](rhuh-outcome-audit.md) binds the actual [TCIA collection](https://www.cancerimagingarchive.net/collection/rhuh-gbm/) and CSV SHA-256 `32d638906d34aaf8f66f5ec41c53c044216aed73bac22c776fb399bf2f741728`.

The cognitive table is the versioned [Figshare baseline/T0/T3 release](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.28001120.v1), keyed by its collection-scoped `ID`. The [audit](glioma-connectivity-outcome-audit.md) found all 63×31 entries present and numeric values finite; undocumented sentinels remain possible. `radiotherapy`/`chemotherapy` describe treatment between visits; `tumor_grade` uses postoperative pathology: exclude them from presurgical predictors. Nine test variables underlie the paper's threshold but only seven domain scores are released, so disagreement does not identify which labels are correct. Connectivity preprocessing also pooled response functions across sessions including T3; preoperative acquisition does not establish prospective processing.

The [BTC audit](btc-longitudinal-outcome-audit.md) retains all 28 exact headers and hashes; preserve `RVP_latancy_mean`, including its source spelling. The paired five-test schema comes from [OSF 5KFW3](https://doi.org/10.17605/OSF.IO/5KFW3), `Legend.csv` v1 and the `Data.csv` v2 header. `_t1/t2` denotes separate columns, not a combined score. Assessment dates do not establish planning availability. Neither OpenNeuro visit IDs nor OSF IDs have a verified individual crosswalk yet; no endpoint rows are admitted by this schema review.

## Small representation, explicit unknowns

Each retained observation needs: collection/patient/visit identity; exact source field/raw value; source version/hash; domain and scale/test version; documented units/direction; valid baseline link; assessment phase/date at source precision; availability time or null; and missingness/ambiguity. Do not promote calendar-only dates into invented UTC timestamps. Reported severity, persistence and independently observed recovery stay separate. `No` is an observed category; blanks, unavailable fields, unopened protected values and unmentioned domains are not `No`.

Default to nominal source categories. An ordinal analysis requires a separately justified ordering: transient deficit has unspecified severity, while minor/major persistent labels combine severity and course. Neither a total clinical order nor equal harm increments are established. A transparent binary derivative is any recorded postoperative deficit versus `No`, never new, worsened or surgery-caused deficit. Do not reconstruct NANO values.

Keep raw values immutable. Derived changes need both source observation IDs, comparable scale/normalization versions and a pinned rule. Keep unavailable dimensions null with reasons such as `not_collected`, `time_unreported`, `definition_ambiguous`, `source_missing` or `withheld_by_role`; do not fill them with zero or fabricated dates.

## Deployment and the implemented ingestion boundary

Keep this evaluator-owned table outside `CaseData`, `FunctionalEvidence`, actor observations and rewards. Baseline fields are candidates for a separately declared preoperative prediction task; postoperative labels are targets only. Postoperative imaging may be input to a separately named postoperative association task, not a preoperative predictor. All visits and derivatives inherit the patient's frozen role; BTC TRAIN05/16/20/22/25/28, SELECT26/27 and unopened29/31 remain unchanged.

Existing [`ContextField`](../src/resectionlab/core.py) already rejects unknown or post-cutoff availability from primary planning. Relative visit descriptions must not be converted into invented UTC timestamps to bypass it. A future allowed-feature identity must hash only permitted values: retain the full mixed pre/post CSV hash for audit, but do not let changing withheld outcomes alter actor seeds through that container hash. Observational outcome prediction does not establish harm under an unperformed route; clinical plan probabilities remain null.

[`rhuh_outcomes.py`](../src/resectionlab/rhuh_outcomes.py) now provides `load_rhuh_csv(path)` for the pinned release and `parse_rhuh_csv(bytes, expected_sha256=...)` for explicitly bound inputs. It checks the digest before parsing, preserves all 23 exact headers/tokens in frozen records, rejects duplicate/out-of-collection IDs and unknown categories, and keeps pre/post KPS separate. `any_recorded_postoperative_deficit` maps `No` to false and the three recorded deficit categories to true; missing remains null. Assessment/availability timestamps are null, phases source-labelled, and domain, new/worsened status and recovery remain unresolved. Missing-token handling is explicit; it does not establish validity of undocumented source sentinels.

`record.preoperative_projection()` contains only preoperative KPS and contrast-enhancing volume in cm³, with collection/patient/release identity and fixed units/interpretation. Its `semantic_hash` excludes the mixed CSV digest, row order, raw numeric formatting and every excluded field. Full-file provenance remains on the evaluator record/dataset. The projection explicitly sets `primary_planner_authorized=False`; source-relative timing does not bypass `ContextField`. No roles, imaging features, normalization, model fits or reward changes are assigned by this module.

The 39 [owner controls](../tests/test_rhuh_outcomes.py) pass, including excluded-field permutation invariance, missing-versus-`No`, strict source/schema checks, direct-constructor validation and immutable raw snapshots. A read-only pinned-release check returned 40 records and the audited 26/6/6/2 categories with unchanged source bytes. Independent review is separate; no prediction experiment is released by these checks.
