# Fixed TRAIN observation coverage

This prospective, zero-learning experiment compares the unchanged access-centered crop with a permitted-target-centered native crop and a conservative whole-source coarse view. The two new views remain outside the task and policy. No checkpoint is loaded, and no cuts, search, policy forwards, learning or access selection occur.

The fixed denominator is PAT05, PAT16, PAT20, PAT22, PAT25 and PAT28. PAT16/20 retain their historical, source-bound target/support conflicts: their representation fields stay null, with no new image decoding. The other four original preparations are attempted once each. SELECT and unopened subjects are forbidden. Exact original bundles, accesses, support acknowledgments, tools, objective, normalization, native frame and horizon remain fixed. Changing the observation cannot change proposals; physical catalogs, task state and legacy observation fingerprints are checked before and after view construction.

Both new grids are bounded by 64³. The local rule uses the covered positive permitted nominal bounding-box midpoint; oversized or missing targets are disclosed or rejected. The global rule preserves source cell extent and volume through cell-overlap averages. Missing functional channels remain unavailable. Input values, coverage and provenance remain distinct.

For each original emitted proposal, report accepted/rejected status, the current five entry-to-tip samples, and continuous shaft-centerline visibility at approach and deepest poses. The shaft extends from tip minus working length to tip minus active-tip length. Center interpolation bounds and full-cell extents are separate. Fractional coverage stays fractional. Centerline visibility does not establish full instrument-volume visibility or clearance.

Freeze error gates before images: extent/roundtrip ≤1e−8 mm; global nominal mass relative error ≤1e−6, or absolute error ≤1e−6 mm³ below 1 mm³. Report actual errors, target overflow, construction time and worker RSS. Improved coverage is not a pass criterion.

One CPU, 6 GiB sampled RSS, 170 s cooperative/174 s supervisor cutoff within a 180 s envelope; ≤78 initial previews per attempted case, ≤312 total. Preserve all failures and partial receipts; no retries. Root commits reviewed source, then the source-bound declaration, then creates the immutable archive and explicit release. The runner verifies archive commit, manifest and numerical bytes before execution.
