# Saved-output checks for the numerical tet10 cube

Status: **source-only and not executed**. `scripts/mechanics_nonpatient_sparse_output.py` checks supplied FEBio text for one of the seven frozen 10 mm cube cases. The parser does not open a file, start FEBio, bind an executable or backend, or inspect patient anatomy. Its numerical Ogden gauge is a software fixture, not a measured brain property. No result from this module validates tissue retraction, cutting force, patient deformation, injury, or surgical decisions.

The accepted text grammar is based on the repository's saved FEBio 4.13 tet10 control: `*Step`, `*Time`, `*Data` and 1-based comma-separated node/element rows; `Nonlinear solution status`, a `convergence norms: INITIAL CURRENT REQUIRED` header, one residual row for the final status, a line-anchored `------- converged at time`, mesh counts, and a final `N O R M A L   T E R M I N A T I O N` in the solver log. All initial and declared load states, exact row IDs and columns, finite fields, synchronized times, matching mesh counts, every convergence token, exact saved `dtol`, `etol`, `rtol` headers of `1e-10`, an exact `min_residual` header of `1e-20 N²`, and a final reported residual under the frozen requirement are required. Duplicate unfinished status, missing or duplicated residuals, malformed convergence, tolerance or residual-like lines, a premature normal-termination marker, and explicit error text fail. FEBio's distinct `evaluating residual` timing line remains accepted. A zero process exit is insufficient. Another native output grammar must be reviewed explicitly; the parser will not infer missing convergence evidence.

For every state, the validator reconstructs dimensionless `det(F)` from logged current positions at all eight pinned TET10G8 points, vertices, edge midpoints and centroid. It checks the element-logged `J` against the arithmetic mean of the eight Gauss values, verifies `x=X+u`, checks the declared prescribed boundary motion, and independently sums signed nodal reactions for net force and **current-coordinate** moment balance. Nonzero reactions at unconstrained nodes are rejected even if their total force and moment cancel. At the unloaded initial state, `x≈X`, `u≈0`, `J≈1`, stress and energy density near zero, and **every** nodal reaction near zero are required; balanced preload on constrained nodes cannot pass by cancellation. The signed top and bottom aggregate reaction vectors are retained for the separately frozen cross-mesh convergence check. The existing declaration's `1e-9 m` affine motion allowance is also used conservatively for nonuniform motion consistency because it declares no distinct nonuniform boundary tolerance; this is an output-level software gate, not a physical error bound. The initial-state, force, moment and `J` gates use the declaration's preexisting thresholds.

The parser consumes only caller-supplied ASCII strings and rejects combined input above the declared 512 MiB active-output cap, a solver log above 16 MiB, or lines above 2,048 characters. Its returned `passed` means these **saved-output** checks passed. It does not establish a native solve, runtime/backend selection, source/deck/output hashes, independent affine stress or energy agreement, field convergence across mesh levels, resource-cap compliance, or physical fidelity. Those remain separate release and validation dependencies. In particular, the saved `J` and balance checks cannot show that a fabricated internally consistent field solves the finite-element equations; the native residual and independent analytic/convergence checks must be coupled to a provenance-bound supervised run.

`tests/test_mechanics_nonpatient_sparse_output.py` uses explicitly synthetic text fixtures. It checks complete parsing and deliberate failures for missing states, altered row IDs, nonfinite values, wrong labels/times/counts, missing or misleading convergence, duplicated or unfinished residual status, changed solver tolerance headers, explicit error text, invalid boundary motion, altered `J`, net imbalance, and self-canceling reactions on free or initially constrained nodes. These tests are parser controls only; no native output has been obtained for the seven-case benchmark.

Source-only validation passed 80 adjacent tests. The parser also read the previously saved 27-node/six-tet `tet10_affine` FEBio 4.13 control: five node states, five element states, and four residual-qualified convergence tokens. That readback directly checks the mesh-count, tolerance-header and `converged at time` grammar in a saved native log. It is not a new solve or evidence about the larger cube cases.
