# Case4 boundary-6 mesh candidate — source-only preparation

This separate v4 candidate requests a 6 mm boundary and 24 mm interior size through the existing Gmsh discrete-surface `Distance+Threshold` field, with the same 2–24 mm transition and Sampling 100 as v2. Curvature sizing stays off. It uses straight-reference tet10 and the same retained SynthStrip T1 mask, native T1 affine, saved surface, metre conversion, classification, parametrization, and geometry validators. The 6 mm request is a hypothesis about v2's surface-chord error, not a measured accuracy or runtime result.

The unchanged physical gates require complete source and returned manifold/topology checks, shared midpoint identity within 1e-12 m, positive tet10 Jacobian witnesses, mean ratio at least .05, no positive-volume overlap, both full-triangle surface-distance upper bounds at most 2 mm with at most 1 mm covering radius, and relative enclosed-volume error at most 3%. These compare a proposed mesh to an estimated source envelope. They do not validate anatomy, displacement, forces, resection or patient safety.

One generation and zero retries are permitted only after a separate independent source review and root release of a committed ten-file execution closure. The launcher verifies that closure against the immutable source commit and checks the retained source/runtime/QC and prior-failure evidence before the patient surface is decoded. It uses a fresh output directory and supervises one numerical thread, 180 s, 3 GiB sampled process-group RSS, 32 MiB total output, 8 MiB per file, and 32 files. The candidate count ceiling is 64,000 nodes/80,000 tet10 elements. A complete diagnostic packet is separately bounded at 80,000 nodes/100,000 elements/16 MiB; exceeding a count or byte limit cannot silently truncate it. These allowances are geometry-only and do not admit a patient-size solver.

The v1 coarse count failure, v2 bidirectional surface-fidelity failure, and v3 incomplete 180 s curvature attempt remain terminal and unchanged. A v4 timeout, count, topology, Jacobian, overlap, surface or volume failure must be recorded as a failure. A changed profile or retry requires a new declaration and review. No B/V destinations, during-US, cavity label, solver, training or policy input is part of this mesh preparation.

Preparation controls use generated analytical fixtures only. They challenge the exact 6/24 mm field, curvature-off options, one charged generation, source/config immutability, unchanged gates, packet/output bounds, and release refusal.
