Current conclusion

Keep development_episode's existing statement: probe records geometric contact, not new anatomical knowledge; SEARCH may omit its cost under the unchanged removal objective. In the current complete generated fixture, a probe changes observed contact history, current tool, budget and state-bound IDs, but not tissue, cavity, connected free space, images, nominal target or the physical feasible inventory. The contact is a deterministic consequence of already public support/tool geometry, not a new measurement. With nonnegative effort/change costs and no contact utility, removing probe actions preserves any subsequent physical aspiration sequence while saving cost and decisions. The numerical witness saves 0.186 return with exactly the same final removed mask. This is a supported model statement, not clinical advice about probing.
