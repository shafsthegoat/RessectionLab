# Saved HBE cross-mesh diagnosis

The original experiment remains **failed before calibration or held-out-response access**. Its 8 µm displacement limit, reaction trend rule, material, loads and boundaries are unchanged. This post hoc diagnosis reads only saved mesh manifests, solver readouts, endpoint node records and their frozen readout source; it creates no meshes or new solutions.

The displacement failures reflect unresolved spatial discretization, with no evidence of a probe-map defect. Independent bilinear-XY/linear-Z interpolation at the same 75 rest points reproduces all saved endpoint probes within 5.85×10⁻¹⁴ m. Alternative valid host cells agree within 1.51×10⁻¹⁶ m. The largest rest-coordinate reconstruction residual is 4.25×10⁻¹³ m. These errors are negligible beside the 22.356/23.258 µm compression/tension differences. The criterion is the maximum Euclidean displacement difference across all 75 points and 61 common load states, not an RMS or an error against continuum truth.

Both worst medium–fine differences occur at full load, radius 0.75R, on the positive-y axis: compression at z=0.25H, tension at z=0.75H. N=4/8/12 has 2/4/6 axial layers. Quarter-height points lie on N8 layer planes but halfway between N12 planes; legitimate hex8 interpolation therefore contributes to the comparison. It cannot explain the result as merely a sampling artifact: midplane probe changes are still 18.878/18.083 µm; 56/75 and 66/75 endpoint probes exceed 8 µm. Shared non-end-plane nodes, with no probe interpolation, change by as much as 28.243/25.124 µm. These shared free nodes include the lateral surface; the compression maximum is strictly inside the cylinder. The pointwise finite-element fields themselves change broadly.

Tensile force is monotone across meshes: 27.244732, 27.045248, 26.817506 mN. What fails is decreasing *change magnitude*: 0.199484 then 0.227743 mN, although the latter is only 0.849% of the fine force. The meshes also approximate different polygonal domains: area/ideal-circle area is 0.974495, 0.993587, 0.997147. As a diagnostic only, dividing force by these ratios gives changes 0.737972 then 0.325569 mN. This supports competing geometric and field-discretization errors as a plausible reason for the small coarse–medium difference; it does not isolate causality or justify area-correcting acceptance. Step and modulus-scale success cannot establish mesh accuracy.
