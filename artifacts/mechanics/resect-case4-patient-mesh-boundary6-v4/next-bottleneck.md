# Case4 v4 source-only mesh: next-bottleneck audit

Read-only scope: frozen source/manifests and saved JSON metadata for v1–v4. I did not open the patient mask, image, retained surface arrays, B/V landmarks, or during-US data; I did not run Gmsh or a solver. This is an engineering diagnosis, not an anatomical or mechanics validation.

## What the saved result establishes

- v1: a 24 mm coarse request returned 2,065 nodes and 1,052 tets, exceeding its 2,000-node cap before geometry/fidelity assessment. v3: curvature sizing timed out at 180.03 s before a complete volume candidate; it supplies no fidelity comparison.
- v2 (12 mm boundary / 24 mm interior) returned 5,223 nodes and 2,761 tet10. v4 (6 mm boundary / 24 mm interior, curvature off) returned 19,070 nodes and 10,512 tet10: **3.65× nodes and 3.81× elements**. v4 finished in 17.63 s at 591 MB sampled group RSS. It passed the recorded topology, tet10/Jacobian, midpoint, overlap, mean-ratio, and volume checks, but its final status is **failed_or_incomplete** because both independent surface-fidelity directions failed. No solver was called or authorized.
- Source→mesh sampled maximum fell 5.460→3.869 mm (29.1% lower), while the full-surface upper bound fell 6.167→4.576 mm (25.8% lower). Mesh→source sampled maximum fell 3.834→2.264 mm (41.0% lower); its upper bound fell 4.831→3.254 mm (32.6% lower). Both v4 upper bounds remain above the frozen **2.000 mm** gate by 2.576 and 1.254 mm respectively. Even the sample maxima exceed 2 mm. Relative enclosed-volume error improved 1.208%→0.315%, below the 3% gate. Mesh boundary area remains 10.44% below the estimated source area (v2: 12.42% below); neither area figure proves anatomy fidelity.
- The v2 saved diagnostic established that its boundary corner vertices were on the source, while some midsides and face centroids were >2 mm away. This supports a straight-chord contribution to v2 error. The v4 result has no saved per-patch or localized witness breakdown; it does **not** establish that the remaining v4 error comes from the same locations or exclusively from chord approximation.

## Why another global refinement is a poor immediate bet

At 19,070 nodes, the unreduced displacement system has 57,210 DOFs. The code's conservative dense-profile Skyline estimate is 13.09 GB for one symmetric value array and **39.28 GB for three** (36.58 GiB), before other allocations. The latter is 12.19× the 3 GiB geometry-process cap and 13.33× the corresponding v2 value. This is a memory warning, not a measured solver requirement: sparse storage or constraints can change it, and no patient-size sparse solve has been benchmarked. However, simply tightening the global boundary size to chase the 2 mm surface gate is likely to add nodes to a mesh already unusable by the currently characterized Skyline route. The 6→12 mm count/error response is not enough to extrapolate a successful size, memory footprint, or runtime.

**Recommendation: stop Case4 global mesh refinement for now.** Do not relax the 2 mm gate, accept the rejected v4 mesh, project midsides as a shortcut, or start Case4 FEM/policy use. The source is an estimated T1 envelope with known inferior/cerebellar omissions and no anatomical registration acceptance; even a perfect mesh of it would not validate true tissue geometry or retraction forces. Case4's 13 V outcomes were previously exposed, so a later displacement comparison on it is retrospective DEVELOPMENT only.

## Testable route to reconsidering a mesh attempt

The narrow hypothesis is that the remaining source→mesh discrepancy is concentrated on a small fraction of high-curvature source patches, so *prospectively frozen local* refinement could improve the worst-case bound with much less node growth than another global halving. First, independently audit saved v4 diagnostic arrays against the retained source under a separate release: map >2 mm witnesses and upper-bound contributions by patch/area, and test whether a small predeclared region contains the extreme error. Keep B/V and during-US sealed. A diffuse error field falsifies the local-refinement premise and should end that path. This diagnostic is a proposed experiment, not an observed result or authorization to open source arrays in this review.

Before any new Gmsh attempt, independently gate (1) anatomical suitability of the fixed source envelope for the intended conditional-displacement task; (2) a patient-size **sparse** solver memory/convergence profile on a separate non-patient or otherwise authorized mesh, with an explicit budget and numerical accuracy checks; and (3) one frozen local-size-field declaration with one generation, no retry, exact source/runtime hashes, untouched 2 mm bidirectional full-surface bounds and 3% volume gate, topology/Jacobian/overlap checks, and the same no-solver/no-clinical-admission flags. A new mesh must show both fidelity gains **and** a plausible solver-memory route; a geometry-only pass cannot license deformation, force, injury, or policy claims.
