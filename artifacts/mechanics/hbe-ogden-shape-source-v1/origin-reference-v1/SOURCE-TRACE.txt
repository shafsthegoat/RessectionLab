Reference/loading semantics and magnitude of the local signed-force deviation

Conclusion: keep the tested optional alpha capability, but no fitted physical
improvement claim yet. The local signed-force feature is small relative to the
large existing model mismatch. It is a property of a processed cyclic average;
it does not establish a negative physical tangent on an initial loading ramp,
a faulty instrument, or an identifiable force offset. No noise bound follows.

3. What the exposed 60 saved samples show
Neither branch contains exact u=0. Closest points are -13.110338983 um and
+11.860338983 um. Their 24.970677966 um gap matches the near-origin within-branch
spacing. Together with 30+30 rows, this is consistent with an even 60-point grid
across the whole compression/tension mode; it does not prove the original
instrument lacked an unloaded reference. No zero-force value can be recovered
from this saved grid without an additional assumption or extrapolation.

Tensile peak is 6.744340386 mN. All four negative forces occur at the first four
positive coordinates; magnitudes relative to that peak are:

  u 61.8017 um: F -0.340868 mN, 5.054135%

They are small localized signed deviations, not a global sign reversal. For
scale, the unchanged common-mu baseline's weighted tensile RMSE reproduces as
8.641906583 mN, 128.135682% of measured peak. The earlier 5.78046 ratio of independent
branch amplitudes remains an important same-data model-adequacy diagnostic.
These local deviations do not by themselves explain that much larger mismatch.

4. Knowable limits and next interpretation
Filtering/hysteresis averaging, a reference-position/tare residual, and small
measurement errors are compatible explanations; the available metadata cannot
distinguish or quantify them. A constant force shift would leave the observed
negative finite interval slopes unchanged. Those slopes are not infinitesimal
moduli or measured evidence of living-tissue instability. No points should be
dropped or offset fitted in response to this inspection.
A later shared-alpha fit could be reported as improvement of this processed
DEVELOPMENT proxy conditional on the fixed boundaries/geometry, while retaining
all points and separately displaying the origin residual. It could not resolve
the unknown reference/noise semantics, count as independent donor/mode testing,
or validate living retraction/cutting/patient properties. No alpha fit or native
solve was performed here, and the original failed torsion attempt is unchanged.
