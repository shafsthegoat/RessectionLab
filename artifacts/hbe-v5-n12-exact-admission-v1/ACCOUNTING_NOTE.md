# Scope of combined timing

The combined 25.71928400127217 seconds is the sum of recorded native, readout and preparation stages, including the separately measured saved replay. N8 has a recorded native runtime but lacks its historical readout/preparation durations. Those unknown durations are not measured zeros. Therefore this number is a **known-stage subtotal**, not complete end-to-end elapsed cost and not evidence of a speed advantage. Closed output bytes include the known original and supplemental inventories.

The numerical exception does not repair the original N12 missing post-run guard observations; the failed original and separate supplement remain distinct.
