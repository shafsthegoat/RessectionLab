## Downstream admission boundary

The original parent failure occurred before it wrote `source_hashes_after` or `runtime_profile_verified_after`. Replaying today verifies unchanged saved inputs and current source/runtime identities, but cannot reconstruct those missing historical post-run observations. Therefore the original failed receipt must remain a failed attempt, and generic `validate_prior_chain`/twelve-row comparator admission should remain closed. A future row may use this **specific** supplement only through a separately reviewed, exact-hash exception that binds both receipt hashes, both releases, all nine original file hashes, the replay hash, and this explicit historical gap. Such admission must label the N12 evidence as recovered saved-output numerical evidence, preserve the failed original, and avoid any physical-force or clinical inference. No such admission is asserted by this report.
