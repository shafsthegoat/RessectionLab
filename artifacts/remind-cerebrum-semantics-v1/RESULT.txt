Cerebrum annotation is not established total physical tissue occupancy
October 10, 2026

A source-meaning issue constrains the current geometric environment. ReMIND
provides an automatic cerebrum object and a separate manual whole-tumor object.
Its methods identify Brainlab Elements/Object Manipulation, but no exact version
or per-case object generation order. Whole tumor differs from the intended
resection target; enhancing-lesion annotation includes any necrotic core.
Source: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-024-03295-z (Segmentation data; Data export).

A manufacturer-authored historical guide documents a plausible reason for their
non-overlap. Brainlab iPlan Stereotaxy3.0, Software User Guide Rev1.4 (2014),
printed page174, section13.4, describes atlas segmentation that excludes an
outlined tumor region; tumor is also ignored if not outlined first. This is
workflow evidence, not proof of the exact ReMIND version, procedure or accuracy.

In the unchanged world, removal is a subset of cerebrum support S. The fraction
of the full supplied target T removed therefore cannot exceed |S intersect T|/|T|,
even before access/tool restrictions. Existing TRAIN tasks exclude34.55%,45.95%,
81.04%,42.02% of their targets from simulated occupancy. OnSELECT013 this ceiling
is4.48%. This is an environment assumption, not evidence that those source tumor
regions are empty, impossible to remove clinically, or wrongly registered.

The original partial-target baseline remains intact. The next source-prepared
TRAIN-only sensitivity contrast compares S against explicit assumed simulated
occupancy S union T, with original masks/full target/domain retained and baseline
access fixed. Union assumes all labeled tumor, including unseparated necrotic
components, behaves as occupied removable cells. It is not corrected anatomy,
measured tissue composition, neurological safety or physically validated surgery.
No filling, dilation, clipping or inferred intensity-based anatomy is proposed.

Existing support-dependent MRI normalization is a coupling: the first contrast
is search-only and records this effect. Any later neural comparison needs shared
normalization or explicit coupling disclosure. Derived occupancy must have its
own provenance and truthful extent flag. The staged paired recipe specifies
this prospective contrast; no union construction or patient experiment has run.
SELECT/EVAL are excluded and existing SELECT outcomes are not retuned.
