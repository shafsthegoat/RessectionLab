Proposal: one fixed full-teacher IL fit, not a new benchmark

TRAIN evidence motivating this change: the original actor is near-uniform and
never chooses STOP. After the first IL pilot, fixed40-state CE changes only
1.861780 -> 1.822646; movement32 CE is unchanged (2.053795 -> 2.054069), while
STOP8 improves (1.093719 -> .896954). Conditional movement ranks improve from
1/32 to 22/32, including teacher probe 16/16. This may reflect simple state/mode
cues and is not proof of goal-specific geometric reasoning. STOP remains above
the best movement by .071-.103 on probe states and .256-.287 on aspiration
states. RL instead assigns mean STOP probability .9999908. The two failure
patterns are different. This proposal tests IL fit only, not RL superiority.

One explicit opt-in experiment, generated-public-contact-full-teacher-refit-v1,
binds the complete ordered 40-state corpus and its original teacher bindings,
seals, actions and observation fingerprints. Its default counterpart preserves
the exact original pilot record, protocol, experiment hash, initialization,
architecture and checkpoint identity. The original final files remain intact.

Use fresh scratch initialization with the original architecture/seed/Adam and
unchanged mean categorical cross-entropy. Make 32 optimizer updates; every update
uses all 40 states exactly once in original teacher/action order. Retain the
eight genuine STOP labels as well as 16 aspiration and 16 probe labels. No manual
STOP penalty, STOP masking, class reweighting, new action head, learning-rate
search, extra teacher or alternate layout. Loss and update must remain bound to
this new experiment; a refit checkpoint cannot load as the original pilot.

This costs 1,280 loss forwards versus 128 previously, plus 40 before and 40 after
readout forwards. This is an additional-compute optimization diagnostic, not a
matched-compute improvement claim. Fixed endpoint only, no checkpoint selection.
Read the same 24 TRAIN teacher files and 32 prior IL receipts used by the released
diagnostic. Reconstruct 24 tasks and exactly 40 saved teacher steps, matching
every original observation fingerprint, native history, binding and seal.
All source recipes, roles and objectives stay fixed. No SELECT/MEASUREMENT
task, observation, result or episode reads; prospective family metadata alone
may be checked to recreate the exact experiment identity. No patient reads.

Before and after the 32 updates, read all 40 states in eval/no_grad mode, without
executing any predicted action. Save complete legal masks/logits, STOP/teacher
probabilities, STOP-minus-best-movement margins, exact teacher rank among legal
movement candidates, greedy action/mode and CE. Summarize fixed all 40, root24,
second16, STOP8, movement32, and the 16 aspiration-root states. Report whether
STOP8 remain correct and whether positive-demo roots prefer movement; preserve
regressions and zero-improvement outcomes. Do not infer usefulness from CE alone.

Proposed fit attempt: 180 seconds hard worker wall, 1 GiB sampled owned process
tree, one thread, 2,048 geometry previews, one attempt. Reuse the existing owned
monitor and parent lease. Preserve every update/readout plus partial checkpoint
on a cap, with no retry, budget increase or alternate state. Save final 32 only
under the new experiment lineage. These are prospective bounds, not a released
run. The focused controls are staged and unexecuted, awaiting root release.
No new model forward, native transition or optimizer update has been run.

A separate optional follow-up may execute the refit's fixed final greedy policy
on all 24 TRAIN goals through the shared adapter/replay, with at most 48 actor
forwards and 48 planning steps (plus explicitly counted authoritative replay),
45 seconds, 1 GiB, 2,048 previews, one attempt. Root must release this separately.
It cannot change the endpoint or become a selection pass. This is a TRAIN
usefulness check, not held-out evaluation. Existing exposed measurement results
are not an untouched test set for a tuned successor. No generalization or
clinical claim follows from either phase.
