# Independent scientific challenge: what the mechanics work validates

Read-only review, 2026-10-09 UTC. No patient payload, measured HBE response, native primitive, mesher, or solver was opened or run. This is a scope assessment, not an execution release.

## Decision

**HBE_01_03 v5 is a numerical/material prerequisite, not the requested patient-anatomy interaction proof.** Its proposed 12 same-endpoint FEBio reference solves may repair v3's strict axial-coordinate coverage failure and reproduce conditional branch convergence screens. Even if those pass, a later one-scale fit and held-out torsion read would test processed postmortem specimen force/torque under bonded plates. It would not test live glioma tissue, a retractor/cavity boundary, tool force, cutting, or a model's response to a surgical action. The old global spatial failure remains separate. The proposed 12 analogues consumed ~3,941 s and ~3.52 GB in old-domain runs; taking on this substantial numerical work before a patient-domain displacement result should be treated as a deliberate resource choice, not as the shortest path to the interaction milestone. Source: `build/hbe-branch-calibration-v5-adapter-design-v1/REPORT.md`, `docs/hbe-specimen-mechanics-poc.md`, and the [HBE source study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10511383/).

**The shortest credible result with accessible real patient anatomy is conditional displacement propagation, not controlled retraction.** RESECT supplies preoperative MRI, before/during/after intraoperative ultrasound, and before/during retained-anatomy correspondences for 17 of 23 glioma cases. The paper says landmark Set 2 contains Set 1 and notes that resection destroys some features; they cannot be treated as independent repeated outcomes or forced through a cavity. It does not supply synchronized retractor trajectory/contact/load. ReMIND adds MRI/US and segmentations across 114 surgical patients, but the public collection likewise does not establish synchronized contact and force observations. These are imaging checks of *where visible retained tissue moved*, not causal action-response or cutting-force data. Sources: [RESECT creator paper](https://aapm.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/mp.12268), [ReMIND creator collection](https://www.cancerimagingarchive.net/collection/remind/).

## Narrowest patient-domain experiment

Use the existing **RESECT Case4 DEVELOPMENT** protocol, without changing any roles. In its common verified physical frame, supply six preselected before→during ultrasound motion vectors as observations B; seal the remaining 13 destination coordinates V. Use the real T1-derived, explicitly provisional brain envelope as the rest domain. Freeze before V: the FEBio 4.13 material law and element, homogeneous Poisson-ratio/scaling assumptions, no body load, traction-free exterior, six fixed 5-mm volume-average constraints, registration, three mesh levels, load-step schedule, numerical budget and all baselines. A measured point motion is not a measured 5-mm volume mean, and the boundary condition is *observed motion imposed for interpolation*, not a retractor tool action. Absolute stiffness is unidentifiable from this displacement-only setup and must remain a nondimensional scale; predicted reaction forces are unsupported patient forces. Compare withheld 3D error in millimetres with no-shift, proper rigid, and fixed inverse-distance-squared interpolation using identical six inputs and explicit common-support counts. Require converged solver outputs at fixed physical probes, positive deformation Jacobians, constraint residuals, and independently reconstructed V predictions. Preserve failure if FEM does not beat the strongest simple baseline under the stated uncertainty analysis. Sources: `docs/resect-conditional-displacement-poc.md`, `docs/tissue-mechanics-validation.md`, `docs/patient-displacement-comparison.md`, and [FEBio prescribed-displacement documentation](https://febiosoftware.github.io/febio-docs/features/features/solid_bc_prescribed_displacement/).

This currently **cannot run as a passing physical validation**: no accepted Case4 volume mesh exists. The first mesh exceeded a 2,000-node preparation cap; a later graded mesh passed count/element checks but failed the unchanged 2-mm two-way surface-fidelity gate (5.460/3.834 mm); a curvature candidate timed out before volume meshing. The estimated envelope visibly excludes some inferior/cerebellar anatomy and has no verified pial or cavity boundary. B/V destinations remain sealed, and the analytical observation/interpolation helpers are software controls only. The immediate useful engineering experiment is therefore a *single predeclared, source-only mesh candidate* that improves local boundary approximation without changing the mask or geometric gates, then independent mesh and anatomy QC; no patient solve or V reveal follows a mesh failure. Sources: `artifacts/mechanics/resect-case4-patient-mesh-v1/RESULT.md`, `artifacts/mechanics/resect-case4-patient-mesh-graded-v2/RESULT.md`, `artifacts/mechanics/resect-case4-patient-mesh-curvature-v3/RESULT.md`, `artifacts/mechanics/resect-case4-saved-mesh-diagnostic-v1/RESULT.md`.

## Framework fit and the action-response dependency

Retain **FEBio** for this narrow experiment: pinned local source/runtime, analytical controls, existing tet10/MPC integration and independent readers make it the shortest established-framework path. This is engineering reuse, not a claim that FEBio physics is validated. SOFA better matches future interactive contact/topology changes; FEniCSx offers transparent mixed formulations but requires more contact work; SfePy is a smaller displacement research option. Switching engines now would not supply missing measured retractor motion, force, or independent displacement. The repository's framework comparison and [SOFA hyperelastic component documentation](https://sofa-framework.github.io/doc/components/solidmechanics/fem/hyperelastic/tetrahedronhyperelasticityfemforcefield/) support those role distinctions.

For a genuine **controlled-retraction** fidelity test, need measured tool/retractor surface pose and contact phase, a registration/fixture description, plus independent interior or surface tissue displacement; measured load/torque is additionally needed to claim force prediction. A published brain-phantom experiment used tracked surface motion and CT-visible beads to test interior displacement, but publication alone is not verified public raw access. A separate public MULTIS limb/skin interaction release may support nonbrain contact/load software controls; its retraction trials lack paired independent VIC-3D surface deformation and cannot certify brain mechanics. See [brain-retraction phantom paper](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24293030/), `docs/mechanics-interaction-data-access.md`. Until a suitable raw paired brain interaction dataset or controlled prospective phantom experiment is available, mark surgical action-response as an unresolved data dependency. Do not infer patient-specific material constants from scan intensity, or present a simulated retractor contact scene as observed surgical evidence.

## Recommended sequencing and claim labels

1. Preserve HBE v3 terminal no-fit result; keep v4/v5 explicitly non-executable until numerical source review and a separate bounded release. Its eventual result is *specimen force/torque evidence only*.
2. Resolve Case4 real-anatomy mesh fidelity in one frozen candidate; if it passes, execute the existing six-B/13-V conditional displacement test with three meshes and step refinement, keeping V sealed until the field is frozen. A pass would support *observed-motion-conditioned deformation interpolation on one development patient* only.
3. Seek or generate a separately governed, paired tool-motion/tissue-displacement brain phantom or intraoperative dataset. Only then freeze a contact/retraction model and compare predicted displacements with independent measurements; add measured loads for force validation. Brain-shift images alone do not license cutting-force, injury or action-response claims. Only after that task-specific gate should search/imitation/RL compare choices in the same mechanically validated environment with hidden material and future outcomes excluded from deployment observations.

The project already has the correct three-gate distinction in `docs/tissue-mechanics-validation.md`; the risk is promoting a rigorous *specimen* qualification into a *surgical interaction* milestone. No current local result passes any of the three physical-fidelity gates.
