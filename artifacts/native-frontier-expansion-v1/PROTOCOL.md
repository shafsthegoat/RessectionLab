# Native proposal coverage development probe

This is a post hoc development investigation following the completed v2 study.
Its early probe observed legal deeper and neighboring insertions after the old
four-point inventory had exhausted its actions. The new rule therefore has a
new action-model identity and is not a validation result for the old study.

The experiment imports only that study's preserved source at
`68e4fde13b1fa13411e59af663bd17ae63885947` and checks every retained numerical
source checksum before use. The source patient, assumed support, 6-mm-radius
window, named tool dimensions, 0.25-mm microsteps, physical reward, partial
contact accounting and whole-tool/native containment rules remain fixed.
No optimization, selection, final or stress world partition is opened. No
policy is trained. Missing functional evidence remains missing.

The fixed comparator reuses the original four physical entry/target pairs. The
expanded rule inspects thirteen declared source-grid column offsets within the
same aperture. Each column proposes its distal remaining annotated target cell;
if the native checker rejects that insertion, it tries the proximal remaining
target cell. Every proposal starts at the existing access plane and completes
retraction. The primitive pays for all intervening removal, retains partial
cells and checks the swept shaft against the prior cavity at every microstep.
The generator never opens a corridor or exempts cells from those checks.

Both inventories use the same deterministic nominal greedy selection and caps:
three committed cuts, 128 checked previews and 45 seconds of search. The finite
expanded inventory is annotation-assisted and not globally complete. Different
numbers of checked proposals and different insertion depths are reported;
comparison with the earlier beam-search timing is descriptive only.

Before the patient comparison, test a constructed solid slab and a slab with a
complete forbidden barrier. Freeze full chosen histories before independent
native audits. Report rejected and successful preview denominators, target and
normal removal, partial normal contact, residual coverage, setup/search/audit
times, and any harness or scientific failures. The intended patient result is a
geometric action-coverage diagnosis, not a complete resection plan or evidence
of clinical access safety.

The launcher stopped on a metadata-key typo before the first comparison; the
failed attempt is preserved. Correcting that harness key changes no declared
geometry, candidate rule, source, reward or search budget.
