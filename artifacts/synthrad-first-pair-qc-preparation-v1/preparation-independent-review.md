## Conditions to close before first-pair access

1. **Freeze the actual runner and controls.** The six cited utility modules are building blocks, not a reviewed SynthRAD driver. Bind their exact code/runtime versions, pair allowlist, output paths and all effective bounds. Offline controls must exercise wrong-role or extra-member refusal, changed archive identity, local/central mismatch, bad ZIP CRC, inner-gzip CRC/truncation, decompression/header allocation limits, active/inactive forms, units, scaling, interruption, disk/log/RSS limits, and failure-preserving no-admission receipts. Check and retain stat identity on the open archive descriptor through execution; a path-only precheck has a replacement race.
2. **Resolve privacy-rendering order.** The anatomy stage says render “after privacy gate,” but visual privacy clearance itself requires rendering. Declare two states: restricted local diagnostic views for privacy review, then portable views only after a recorded release disposition. Define restrictive creation permissions/locations and sanitize exceptions/logs. `nifti1_header_record_coded_v2` returns `header_base64`, which contains original text fields; keep that raw record restricted and omit it and suspect strings from portable receipts. Unknown private extensions stay pending. Inspect bounded gzip optional filename/comment/extra fields as well as NIfTI text/extensions; the existing framing helper alone does none of this.
3. **Freeze reproducible statistics and views.** Percentile levels, exact/approximate bounded algorithm and error policy, background/clipping definitions, slice fractions, extent sampling/contact-sheet coverage, display windows and interpolation/orientation conventions are not yet specified. Do not use a full-array percentile implementation under an 8 MiB scalar-workspace claim. Freeze those choices before real pixels, keeping source-scale intensity and display-derived metrics distinct. Missing clinical CT scaling or unknown units remain pending; MRI has no invented universal scale.
4. **Record dispositions precisely.** Define gross correspondence, intracranial coverage and calvarium usability rubrics before the first read, with review_pending for unresolved findings. A finite, nonconstant image can still be corrupt or anatomically unsuitable. First-pair completion requires actual evidence for every claimed stage, not just a process exit or header pass. Full TRAIN expansion needs its separate frozen declaration, unchanged first-pair receipt reuse, preserved failures and all 126 denominator rows.

These are implementation obligations, not requests to change the source split, increase limits, access protected roles, or resolve all scientific admission questions before mechanical TRAIN QC.

## Threshold and resource assessment

The 0.01 mm qform/sform and CT/MR corner tolerance, spacing atol 0.01 mm/rtol 1e-4, direction-Gram tolerance 1e-4, and determinant floor 1e-12 reproduce existing `imaging.py` engineering choices. No source-specific calibration or clinical justification was supplied. They are acceptable as conservative coded-grid/parser checks only. Report residuals and classify a boundary failure as unsupported or review_pending; it is not proof of bad anatomy. Preserve raw headers before any library normalization, validate active transform code semantics, and bind explicit numerical tolerances in implementation. No shear support is an implementation limit, not evidence that sheared data are scientifically invalid.

Time, memory, disk and output caps are operational choices rather than scientific thresholds. The largest declared TRAIN gzip member (20,843,405 bytes) fits the 64 MiB cap. All TRAIN original gzip members total 2,463,706,753 bytes; these plus maximum 16 MiB views and 2 MiB receipts per pair leave room below the 8 GiB incremental campaign cap. Two maximal float32 arrays alone reach the 1 GiB target, so that target is not a full working-set estimate. Rendering/statistics temporaries and library allocations require their own accounting; the 2 GiB polling stop is correctly not represented as an instantaneous OS ceiling. Keep one pair staged at a time, bound before allocation and preserve explicit incomplete outcomes on cap breaches. No timings or decoded-size observations currently justify claiming every pair will fit.

The existing scalar helper streams effective float64 values and checks EOF, but only reports extrema. It does not implement nonconstant decisions, percentiles or privacy review. Its bound keys also differ from the proposal (`max_voxels`, `max_native_payload_bytes` versus the proposal's per-image names), so the reviewed wrapper must translate explicitly. Raw and effective scaling must both be recorded, including inactive slope/intercept encodings. The v2 recorder correctly preserves inactive qform encoding; the extension helper correctly limits its claim to framing. Neither alone is full NIfTI or privacy QC.
