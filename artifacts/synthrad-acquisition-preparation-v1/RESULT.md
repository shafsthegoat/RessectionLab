# Acquired CT/MRI component: prospective archive intake

The official SynthRAD2023 Task1 archive is 14,471,900,926 bytes, with published MD5
`360bc61e2320d5c5a7145454a81c566a`. It contains released acquired clinical CT/MRI
alongside provider derivatives and other anatomy. The archive remains unacquired.
The challenge's synthesis task does not turn the acquired CT/MRI into generated
images; generated synthesis outputs remain excluded.

Before any payload access, 180 provider-listed brain identities are assigned by
fixed within-center hashing to 126 TRAIN, 27 SELECT and 27 MEASUREMENT_EVAL roles.
Related images and derivatives inherit their subject's role. All previous project
roles remain unchanged. Whole-archive byte staging is separate from future permitted
member extraction; protected roles and other anatomy are not admitted for tuning.

CC-BY-NC-4.0 permits this declared noncommercial research scope; commercial-product
rights are unestablished. Glioma diagnosis, source-family overlap, surgical timing,
skull coverage after defacing/cropping and registration accuracy remain unresolved.
The provider's conflicting center-B prose is retained beside its preview/table,
without asserting a correction. Outline masks are not human anatomical labels.

The intended transfer reuses tested verified-TLS and exact-resume primitives, with
one continuous whole-object request and no per-chunk review pauses. Final size and
published MD5 plus measured local SHA256 precede publication. The new runner and
its offline controls must enforce the declared resource, attempt and recovery bounds.
No extraction, image QC, anatomy review, training or scientific admission occurs
in this acquisition phase. Source metadata and exact contracts are preserved here.
