Central test
Training may use privileged simulation anatomy, rewards, critic features and
teacher search. Deployment gets only the declared imaging, permitted estimates,
tools, constraints and action history. Freeze the entire strategy before a
separate evaluator opens independently withheld real anatomy. Test whether the
learned method improves the quality/time frontier over strong observed-model
search, and whether a matched no-privilege control loses that improvement.
Simulator score alone cannot answer this question.

Current executable evidence
The completed prepared comparison contains six prescribed TRAIN cases, four
complete comparisons and twelve accepted histories. Frozen IL lost reward to
greedy on all four completed cases, with lower online cost. PAT05 was its training
case; PAT22/25/28 were TRAIN transfer, PAT16/20 failed support completeness. Each
successful plan made only three actions and removed about 3–6% of target. This is
not evidence of a full operation, independent patient testing or an RL benefit.
The supplied target annotation is also the scoring reference. The source voxel
geometry checker independently implements checks but does not provide independent
anatomical truth. Exact model-derived feasibility masks and exact simulated future
cavity are privileged inference channels if advertised as limited-scan deployment.

Immediate protocol boundary
Keep three isolated objects/processes: privileged training state; deployable
observation and permitted planning model; private reference evaluator. All arms
share the permitted sources, uncertainty, candidate family, tools and budgets.
At deployment, proposals, crop/normalization, access selection, legality masks,
search scores and stopping may not consult private truth. The preoperative cavity
is a prediction from action history, not an observed future cavity. On a fixture,
swapping hidden target/support/vascular/functional truth must leave every planner
input and the frozen full plan unchanged, before and after a cut, while a positive
control changes evaluator scores. Do not censor truth-invalid plans before scoring.

The fastest valid population pilot is MRI-only on BTC, not CT. Fit/freeze anatomy
estimators on TRAIN and reserve held-out source masks for the evaluator after
ancestry/frame/coverage QC. An annotation-assisted comparator is still useful, but
reusing its supplied annotation for scoring establishes only model-conditioned
planning. Unknown vessels, tracts or critical anatomy stay unassessed. Acquired CT
requires a separate admitted cohort and cannot be inferred from MRI support.

Comparators and bounded execution
Retain STOP, untrained/random controls and greedy; compare strong beam/MPC search,
learned-guided search, frozen IL, scratch RL, IL+RL, and paired no-privilege training.
Existing observed_beam_search already supports negative opening prefixes and actor
expansion ordering without using an untrained value head as a search oracle.
The full matched real-patient runner does not yet exist. Choose one primary learned
family on SELECT; do not pick the winning method from final scores. Log simulated
teacher/reward ancestry, offline costs and all actual updates.

For the next TRAIN-only profiling slice, the proposal reuses the historical 90 s /
468-preview ceiling with a smaller 30 s / 156-preview point, one CPU thread and
6 GiB. These are starting feasibility caps, not proven full-strategy budgets.
Check horizons 3/6/12/24 on TRAIN, freeze a supported horizon and all final caps
before held-out access, and retain horizon-truncation residuals. Cold preparation,
estimation, proposal generation, online search/inference/adaptation, replay and
export count toward total online cost; independent reference scoring is separate.
Randomize/counterbalance order instead of claiming speed from fixed-order runs.

Measure a strategy, not a straight route
Save entry/access, ordered complete-tool poses and changes, each cavity/removal,
residual by target compartment, non-target removal and contact separately,
withdrawal, STOP reason, violations, coverage, alternatives/Pareto quality, runtime,
expansions/previews, memory and every failure. Primary geometric utility can retain
the existing declared coefficients; the full metric vector prevents one weighted
score hiding damage. A failed/unaudited plan has null outcome and a failure flag,
not zero. Immediate STOP is a separate real comparator. All patients stay counted.

Add coherent nonidentical uncertainty worlds, separate optimization/selection/test
worlds, and a distinct stress family. Report conditional event counts/intervals,
mean/tail surrogates, ranking instability and abstention coverage/error curves.
Select abstention thresholds only on SELECT. Missing coverage/frame/localization
can force a reason-coded abstention; unavailable hazard data never mean zero risk.
The current native spatial adapter accepts only deterministic seed 0 and rejects
critical-evidence bindings, so these uncertainty metrics are not yet executable.

Claim ladder and falsification
A: Held-out simulator worlds + independent code replay establish model-conditioned
planning. B: Unseen patients + withheld real labels/observations score frozen plans
and test anatomical generalization, conditional on label/frame quality. C: Held-out
force/deformation/cutting measurements and observed clinical outcomes answer their
separate physical/clinical questions. No claim at C follows from success at A/B.
A learned benefit that disappears with limited inputs, private-truth isolation,
strong search, or independent anatomy does not support the hypothesis. Two BTC
transfer patients cannot establish a population benefit. Final patient-level paired
intervals and failure/violation guardrails must follow a prespecified sample rule.
