# Fixed IL/RL pilot: completed, learned policies unsuccessful

Both scratch policies completed 32 updates from identical initialization. The single frozen held-out comparison contains eight generated layouts and two goals per layout. Search reached and retained the goal in 12/16 tasks (6/8 surface and 6/8 deep); IL and RL both selected STOP in every task, matching the zero-return STOP baseline. All 32 SELECT and 64 MEASUREMENT_EVAL method episodes completed; none were discarded or capped. These are 16 paired tasks per method, not 64 independent patients.

Independent saved-result review verifies split membership, source/commit bindings, both checkpoint manifests and hashes, all 64 update chains, all 24 teachers and all 96 online result/episode bindings. Parent runtime was 183.464 seconds at 401.6 MB sampled peak RSS, with clean termination. The explicit 14-file source binding is not a full transitive closure; the later audit is not proof against transient source changes.

TRAIN teachers provide 16 successful aspiration→probe strategies and eight STOP strategies. IL sampled all 40 distinct labels, with 96 non-STOP and 32 STOP draws. RL encountered only one positive-return episode among 128; its last 64 rollouts all selected STOP. Available traces show exploration collapse but do not establish why IL failed. A fixed TRAIN diagnostic is the next learning step; no additional held-out sweep is authorized by this result. Previously evaluated layouts remain reserved and are now exposed for this experiment.

Search and learned methods share anatomy, public inputs, full-tool constraints, reward, two-step horizon and native replay. Their declared compute budgets differ, including teacher search for IL; no equal-total-compute or useful speed advantage is claimed. No patient data were read, and no clinical, physical or unseen-patient generalization follows. Checkpoints and bulk episodes remain outside Git. The full author and independent reports preserve accounting details and negative results.
