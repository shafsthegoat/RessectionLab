# Closest-work audit

Checked October 4, 2026 against the revised source specifications. This is a targeted methods/access audit of P01–P04 and R09, not an exhaustive review or an established novelty claim. Exact-title and DOI searches followed publisher and author-institution links. An unsuccessful retrieval means **not inspected**, not that the paper lacks a feature.

| P01 — Bakhshmand et al., 2017 | Complete primary article, including Methods 3.1–3.3 and experiment/discussion, through [Europe PMC full-text XML](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC5683204/fullTextXML). [Indexed article](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29184656/). | Functional and structural network degree scores are assigned to gray matter regions and streamlines. No performance number from these papers is adopted as a RessectionLab result. Source article licenses do not establish code, checkpoint or patient-data redistribution rights. No source code, weights or paper figures were copied during this audit.

The testable contribution remains a proposed combination: patient-specific optimization with actual policy updates, complete-tool legality, a shared search space, independent geometry, separate optimization/selection/evaluation worlds, and inspectable negative results. Each part needs working evidence before it can support a paper claim. Missing full methods for P02–P04 remain an explicit limitation on novelty screening. Repeat exact-title searches and citation chaining before a manuscript claims novelty; record the version and full-method access status of any newly located source.
