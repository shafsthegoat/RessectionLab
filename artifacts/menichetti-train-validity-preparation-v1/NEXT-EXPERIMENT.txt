Secondary prediction experiment proposal; no execution or response access authorized

Test a narrow rich-training / limited-input claim: parameters learned from all
six training brain groups predict future measured force in another source brain
group after observing only a fixed short force prefix. This is conditional force
forecasting on released sample indices. It is not validation of indentation
depth, contact geometry, hold alignment or a tissue constitutive law. The current
record still lacks the independent timing/depth evidence needed for those claims.
This proposal does not replace controlled mechanical interaction validation and
cannot satisfy that prerequisite for later RL work. See MECHANICAL-QUALIFICATION.txt.

Candidate input/output boundary
  Every trace has 10,350 sample positions. Use its first 1,000 samples as the only
  per-trial response input; predict positions 1,001–10,350. Nominal sample spacing
  is 0.0001 s from creator metadata. Index zero is the first stored sample, not
  asserted physical contact time. Region label is known but models below share
  global time constants; every model receives exactly the same force prefix.
  Freeze this prefix/horizon and the following rule before development/test access.

Fixed model family and controls
  1. Constant-force control: mean of the last 100 prefix samples, continued for
     the whole forecast interval. This is also the no-observed-memory baseline.
  2. Single-exponential control: b + a exp(-t/tau).
  3. Two-exponential candidate: b + a1 exp(-t/tau1) + a2 exp(-t/tau2).
  Choose global time constants using training suffix error only. Candidate tau
  grid is {0.01, 0.03, 0.1, 0.3, 1.0} nominal seconds; the two-term candidate uses
  the ten distinct ordered pairs tau1 < tau2. These are predeclared algorithm
  settings, not published material properties or priors taken from fitted tables.
  Center and standardize exponential basis columns over the fixed prefix time
  grid. Condition b and amplitudes using that trial's prefix only, with mean
  squared prefix residual plus 0.001 times squared standardized amplitudes;
  leave the intercept unpenalized. Permit signed coefficients because force-sign
  convention is unverified. No test suffix may affect parameters, normalization,
  baseline, contact estimate, sign, crop or exclusion rule. These coefficients
  are forecasting nuisance terms, not identified material constants.

Training and scoring
  For each grid candidate, form prefix-conditioned forecasts for training trials,
  and minimize donor-balanced/region-balanced suffix error. Break ties by smaller
  time constant(s) in ascending lexicographic order. Training uses full suffix
  truth; prospective trial conditioning uses only the prefix. Lock chosen grids,
  implementation and predictions before development suffix scoring. Development
  brains are a fixed one-shot diagnostic; do not change the specification in
  response to their results within this experiment. If development fails, keep
  the two test brains sealed rather than spend that holdout on a failed candidate.
  Normalize each trial's suffix RMSE by the RMS force of that region across all
  finite training trials, balancing training brains equally. A zero training
  scale makes that region ineligible under a predeclared recorded rule; establish
  all scales/regions before opening development/test responses. Average trials
  within region, regions within brain, then brains equally. Keep per-brain scores.
  Practical success criterion: the two-term candidate has at least 10% lower
  normalized RMSE than the better control on each of the two test brain groups.
  A zero-error best control cannot be beaten and therefore cannot produce a pass.
  With only two test brain groups, report their separate outcomes; do not imply
  reliable population confidence intervals or count the 119 test columns as
  independent donors. Failure falsifies this particular forecast claim.

Data validity and artifact caveats
  Apply the same frozen numerical-validity rule to every role at its authorized
  stage. Quarantine a whole nonfinite trial; do not impute/crop it. Retain finite
  constant/all-zero trials, negative/mixed signs, and zero/constant tails. Removing
  constant series would favor a relaxation model over the constant-force control.
  The present QC marks possible padding patterns only; it cannot prove padding.
  Report completeness and exclusions without choosing them from prediction error.
  No temporal pattern alone supplies true contact or hold time. Even a successful
  forecast remains subject to unknown sampling/cropping provenance and overlap
  across public releases. Preserve protected-role conflicts if biological aliases
  are later discovered. Existing HBE/Greiner/patient assignments are unchanged.
