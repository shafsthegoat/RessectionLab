Mechanical qualification remains unresolved; forecast is secondary

The available creator record supports force units, nominal sampling frequency,
brain/region/trial organization and public experimental methods. The structural
result shows only force matrices. TRAIN-only QC can establish whether selected
training columns are finite and describe zero/sign/constant-tail patterns. It
cannot identify loading history or certify a physical phase from those patterns.

The proposed prefix/suffix forecast is not presently a qualified constitutive
relaxation test. It uses sample-relative time and empirical signed exponentials.
No source evidence establishes that its evaluation interval is a hold at constant
true tissue indentation. Time-constant/amplitude estimates would therefore not
be identified material properties. Success would support transfer of recorded
force dynamics under the dataset's collection conditions. Failure could arise
from an unobserved changing input as well as a deficient forecast model; it would
not by itself falsify a constitutive relaxation law under controlled displacement.

Minimum missing evidence for a qualified relaxation test
  1. For each retained trial, a synchronized actual indentation history, or an
     independently validated reconstruction from measured displacement, surface
     contact reference and probe/apparatus compliance. Nominal stage command
     depth/speed and a programmed hold alone do not establish tissue indentation.
     The record must show the held interval satisfies the predeclared displacement
     tolerance. Do not obtain this tolerance by tuning held-out force predictions.
  2. A creator-authenticated mapping from stored sample rows to physical time and
     loading phases: recording onset, contact reference, ramp onset/end and hold
     onset/end, with cropping, padding, resampling and missing-sample handling.
     Row count 10,350 and stated 10 kHz are consistent with the nominal protocol
     but do not authenticate the 350/10,000 phase partition. Force-derived timing
     estimated from a prefix can condition a predictor; it is not independent
     evidence of actual contact or input motion.
  3. Force baseline/tare, polarity, preload and calibration convention associated
     with those retained rows. Micronewton units are known. A positive/negative
     count does not determine the physical compression convention or baseline.
     Where appropriate, a declared bounded baseline/amplitude nuisance rule may
     use training data or the permitted trial prefix; it must be frozen before
     suffix access and must not be presented as independently measured metadata.

For an absolute contact-force constitutive test, additionally bind the indenter
geometry, specimen/contact geometry and boundary conditions to the forward model
and identify which geometric and material assumptions are actually supported.
Geometry cannot be inferred from force-prefix normalization. The public sphere
radius and nominal command are useful method inputs, not a substitute for the
trial-specific controlled input and contact reference above.

Actionable paths
  First complete the separately reviewed TRAIN-only QC; it may expose numerical
  invalidity requiring the predefined quarantine, but it cannot repair missing
  mechanics metadata. If an existing primary-source loading record or authentic
  acquisition specification supplies items 1–3, bind it and predeclare the physical
  forward model, nuisance treatment, uncertainty and held-out metrics before any
  further response access. If it does not, keep the current dataset's forecast
  experiment explicitly prediction-only and retain mechanical validation as an
  unmet dependency. Do not use published all-trace fits to fill the missing input.
