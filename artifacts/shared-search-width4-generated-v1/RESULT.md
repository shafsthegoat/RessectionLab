It used `beam_width=4`, `max_calls=24`, a 10 s search ceiling and the reviewed 30 s/1 GiB sampled-RSS subprocess guard. It loaded no checkpoint, made no actor forward or training update, and read no patient data. The selected two-stroke history passed the shared native replay and independent geometry check.

The width-4 search evaluated **15 of 24 permitted native transitions**, reached neither call nor time cap, pruned no beam prefixes, and scored **1.100** in the generated simulator (2 mm³ nominal target and 4 mm³ normal tissue removed). The saved width-2 search evaluated 9 transitions, pruned two negative prefixes and selected STOP for **0**. RL256's saved two-stroke rollout scored **1.098**. Changing width alone on this fixed task retained the prefixes that width 2 dropped and produced a better route; this is a narrow search-configuration result, not evidence about unseen patients or general planner superiority. The earlier privileged complete-tree 1.100 reference is not a matched limited-input baseline and did not guide this search.

Timings and sampled memory are single-run diagnostics. No clinical validation is claimed.
