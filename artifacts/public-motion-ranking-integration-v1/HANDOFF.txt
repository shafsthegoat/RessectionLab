LOSS
Let M be legal non-STOP motions, z logits with illegal actions already masked to -inf by unchanged SpatialPolicy, r saved public immediate rewards. On a motion teacher row:
  Lmotion = [logsumexp(zlegal) - logsumexp(zM)]
          + sum_{ri>rj; i,j in M} wij * softplus(zj-zi)
  wij = (ri-rj) / sum_{rk>rl}(rk-rl).
Implementation rescales rewards before gap normalization to avoid large-number overflow. This is one normalized ranking term per state, not an unnormalized sum favoring larger inventories. Exact equal rewards have zero weight. Gap weights are RELATIVE within each state: a roundoff-sized difference receives tiny weight only when larger gaps coexist. If all differences were roundoff-sized, normalization would still give a full ranking term; this scheme does not solve that case. In this frozen corpus all25 motion states have substantive positive reward spread (minimum0.03055312500000007, maximum8.61167949023829), so no such degenerate state is present; see motion-reward-spreads.json. If no positive pair gap (one motion/all tied), the ranking term is zero and only the motion gate remains. On STOP teacher rows, use the exact old -log_softmax(z)[STOP]. Preserve .5 mean over 25 motion rows + .5 mean over 4 STOP rows. No between-patient reweighting.

Old motion CE equals the motion gate plus conditional winner CE. Only the latter changes. Therefore STOP's direct logit derivative at fixed logits is unchanged, and STOP-row forward/backward is exact old CE. Shared-encoder/actor gradients change and can change future STOP behavior; this is not a stopping-invariance claim. Immediate-reward ordering is a local teacher objective, not global return optimality or guaranteed better trajectories. The unchanged greedy teacher never explores negative preparatory moves.
