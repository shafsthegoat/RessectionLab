# Physical interaction data follow-up

Checked 2026-10-09 UTC against creator repositories and primary studies. This was a bounded read-only metadata and small-byte-range audit, not bulk acquisition or a claim that no private dataset exists. The [earlier brain-interaction audit](../open-neurosurgery-interaction-data-audit-v1/RESULT.md) remains the broader baseline.

**Decision: direct brain action–force–independent-displacement validation remains HOLD.** The newly identified open sources support narrower engineering or specimen-law checks. They do not provide a registered, same-trial live-brain tool trajectory, relevant measured load, and independent tissue motion.

| Source and exact access check | What it could validate | What it cannot validate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Wang et al. 2026 primary study](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11548-026-03583-6), [creator Zenodo release](https://zenodo.org/records/19370452), [creator code README](https://gitlab.com/nct_tso_public/smallintestine-force-estimation/-/raw/main/README.md). Zenodo API metadata reports **open, CC BY 4.0**, 50 ZIPs (~15 GB) of physical **silicone small-bowel phantom** pulls. The official paper describes robot motion, dual load cells, synchronized monocular video and five phantom geometries. A bounded HTTPS `206` byte-range inspection of `1.zip` read its 128-KiB ZIP directory and small CSV/member headers only. It confirmed `ur5e_state.csv` with timestamp, robot pose and translation fields; `force.csv` with timestamp, two sensors and summed/filtered force; `dataset.csv` with frame–force pairs; plus video/frames. Provider MD5 for the 332,565,462-byte ZIP is `24c4c4b051d4801693945d0cbed0be54`; the full ZIP and MD5 were **not** verified locally. | After selective checksum, calibration, synchronization and geometry-held-out QC, this could test **nonbrain pull-axis force and visual response** under observed robot motion. | No independent **3-D tissue displacement field** is established by the inspected release. The study uses monocular input and notes missing depth/surface reconstruction. Video-derived 2-D apparent motion would not be independent volumetric ground truth. It cannot validate brain retraction, cavity mechanics, cutting, injury or patient material properties. |
| [Greiner et al. 2024 primary study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11620825/), [creator Zenodo v1 release](https://zenodo.org/records/13946193). Zenodo API reports **open, CC BY 4.0**, two CSVs of cyclic loading and compression/tension relaxation from **postmortem human** visual-cortex and corona-radiata specimens of one donor: 61,352 bytes, MD5 `d7049005c42eb40b240a93e5eea75caf`, and 57,025 bytes, MD5 `8e8e1100d4e1188768a34b4374c44988`. Metadata only was checked; payloads were not opened. | A separately frozen specimen material/fixture model could be challenged on measured loading modes after numerical convergence and prospective specimen/fit roles. The paper itself cautions that inverse material parameters can be poorly identified. | One donor and plate loading do not establish live glioma properties, surgical contact, tool force, independent tissue displacement or patient transfer. |
| [Menichetti et al. 2020 creator Mendeley release](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/7sjcn229cw/1), [primary study](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijengsci.2020.103355). The repository labels the data **CC BY 4.0** and describes a `.mat` file of force-relaxation microindentation curves, indexed by specimen and 12 postmortem human brain regions; the paper reports 595 indentations under traumatic-impact-like dynamic loading. Repository description only was checked; payload was not opened. | A specific microindentation/relaxation implementation could be tested if the indenter, units, loading history and specimen grouping are reconstructed before fitting. | These µN-scale indentation tests are not brain retraction or cutting, do not include an independent 3-D displacement field, and cannot supply patient-specific surgical-force constants. |

The [live-swine retraction study](https://www.nature.com/articles/srep28654) used measured retractor-surface displacement as a boundary condition and separate CT bead locations to assess internal displacement, but this check found no verified downloadable individual CT/LRS/bead trials with a synchronized measured tool-force history and reusable data rights. [RESECT](https://archive.sigma2.no/dataset/5D6BFC33-F58D-4F56-88E8-C40AF269D6F2) can test retained-anatomy displacement after its existing anatomy/frame gates; [HBE](https://zenodo.org/records/8095559) can test a postmortem specimen force law. Neither supplies a same-trial brain surgical action–force–displacement benchmark. Do not join measurements across unrelated people, specimens or procedures.

The unresolved dependency is a source-authenticated brain trial with pre-action anatomy, synchronized calibrated tool pose/contact footprint and relevant interface load, **separate registered tissue motion**, support and timing metadata, measurement uncertainty, rights and a frozen held-out split. Until then, the bowel release is an engineering analogue only; specimen curves are material-model evidence only; simulated forces or visuals are not observed surgical outcomes.
