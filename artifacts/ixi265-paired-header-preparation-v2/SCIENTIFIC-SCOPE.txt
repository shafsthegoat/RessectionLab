IXI265: one prospective TRAIN paired-header pilot

Selection uses only the frozen person manifest: choose the smallest existing
split_sha256, then exact subject, among the 70 annotation_available TRAIN people.
This selects IXI:265, source site Guys, filename acquisition code 0845. The rule
does not inspect images, image-header quality, labels or outcomes. A failure
preserves the person's frozen role and does not trigger a replacement selection.

Exact selected source members
T1: IXI265-Guys-0845-T1.nii.gz, 6,980,629 bytes, TAR header offset 1,867,056,128.
MRA: IXI265-Guys-0845-MRA.nii.gz, 14,307,469 bytes, TAR header offset 3,578,892,288.
Both body offsets are header offset + 512. Their exact 512-byte header SHA256 values
are bound by the frozen manifest and the saved complete metadata-only TAR walk.
Label: vessel_dataset/IXI265.nii.gz, identified by the publisher's ZIP preview;
its exact local compressed sizes/CRC/offset remain prospective until the verified
ZIP's central directory is selectively inspected. No rounded preview size is
treated as an exact size or checksum.

T1 and MRA are acquired measurements. The vessel mask is a source-derived
SynthStrip/Frangi/manual-refined reference with incomplete sensitivity/coverage.
Source prose on surgeon review is inconsistent; local manual review has not
occurred. Acquired copies and derived-reference copies stay in different output
directories. No resampling, thresholding, cropping, model execution or source
rewrite is included. Labels do not make unlabelled voxels proven vessel-free.

Matching source ID/site/acquisition-code strings support source-person linkage.
They do not establish acquisition simultaneity, elapsed time, preoperative
availability, common grid or accepted registration. File mtime and filename
acquisition code are never interpreted as acquisition dates. Acquisition time
and cross-modality time interval remain null. The cohort's unresolved biological
aliases/pretrained exposure and protected-role conflict rules remain in force.

Header-only scope
Only NIfTI header and extension-marker bytes are decompressed (352 for NIfTI1,
544 for NIfTI2). The established header helper retains source units and separate
qform/sform codes and matrices, reports missing/invalid/conflicting transforms,
and never silently supplies a fallback frame. Exact declared-grid comparisons
are metadata facts, not anatomical alignment decisions. Voxel arrays, extension
content, gzip end-of-stream integrity and complete voxel payload length are not
validated by this header-only step. Opaque copied-member fixity is separate.

The later intended-use admission helper still requires anatomy QC, coverage/
support evidence, an accepted transform, source availability and a declared
T1-only task. This pilot leaves those not_run/null/false. MRA and labels remain
private reference channels; this recipe adds no actor inputs or prediction,
learning, selection or evaluation permission.

Transport/fixity boundary
All three archives must first have completed their separate opaque verified
intake. The raw archive MD5 reference is the pinned maintained TorchIO downloader,
not an IXI-publisher checksum; label ZIP MD5 comes from its versioned Zenodo
record. Full archive SHA256 comes from that intake. Before release, root must
bind the completed receipt and exact published-file stat tuples. The selective
reader checks regular read-only descriptor identity before/after use. It does
not reopen 17 GB of unrelated member bodies for another full-archive checksum.
Consequently whole-archive fixity is inherited, with metadata continuity checks,
not independently re-established by this pilot. Selected copied bytes receive
their own streaming SHA256 and post-write rehash. Original archives stay intact.

Root alone may authorize actual execution after independent review, full archive
completion and exact runtime release. Use external process supervision covering
the whole worker at 35 seconds, 512 MiB sampled group RSS and 32 MiB output, plus a
declared bounded cleanup reserve. One fresh output directory is one attempt;
failure is preserved and no retry is implicit. The false prepared recipe is not
an execution release. No archive or patient data was opened in preparation.
