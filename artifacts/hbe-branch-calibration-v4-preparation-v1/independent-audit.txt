2. B1 -- Six reference calls do not reproduce the v3 qualification contract

The proposal requires compression N32/S60, N36/S60, N36/S120 and tension
N16/S60, N24/S60, N24/S120. These six calls can test individual solver physics,
one adjacent spatial difference and one finest temporal difference per branch.
They cannot compute a spatial trend from three distinct meshes, the compression
two-latest-limit envelope, order drift or the tension co-primary triplet
classification margins. The proposal correctly disclaims continuum convergence,
but saying that only load/support and reference bindings change is still false:
the path from passing numerical checks to fitting is materially weakened.

This is demonstrated by this project's own saved failure. N32 passed adjacent
force change (0.138140 mN against 0.724169 mN) and probe change (1.727312 um
against 8 um), while its two-limit remaining envelope was 0.985189 mN and failed.
The original N4/N8/N12 study also failed compression motion 22.356144373333107 um
and tension motion 23.25768976023929 um against 8 um, plus tension reaction
trend 0.22774250190189804 mN against the preceding 0.19948362350659735 mN.
Later N12/N16/N24 compression failed raw reaction trend despite passing adjacent
absolute limits. None of these failures can be replaced by a passing pair.

V3 qualify() requires the independently reviewed N36 temporal candidate, whose
comparison also preserved the conditional envelope/classification safeguards,
and the independently reviewed tension temporal candidate. The new-domain six
curves cannot inherit these properties from old +/-15% curves. Old-state reuse,
appending one endpoint, or interpolating a missing coarse curve is inadequate.

Constructive correction A: keep the six-call design diagnostic-only, with no
transition to fitting or torque access regardless of a diagnostic pass. Name the
changed evidence standard explicitly; do not describe it as v3-equivalent
qualification. A stronger design must be declared before any adaptive extra run.

Constructive correction B: before any v4 native work, select and preregister the
following 12-call design. It is the minimum set that supplies every input of the
existing prior-comparable branch comparison contracts without dropping a level:

  Compression endpoint -0.00073726 m:
  N8/S60, N12/S60, N16/S60, N24/S60, N32/S60, N36/S60, N36/S120.
  Tension endpoint +0.0007360099999999 m:
  N8/S60, N12/S60, N16/S60, N24/S60, N24/S120.

Relative to the proposal, add compression N8/S60, N12/S60, N16/S60, N24/S60;
add tension N8/S60 and N12/S60. Preserve the existing representation map:
compression N8/N12/N16 full native, N24/N32/N36 native lower-half with full
reconstruction; tension N8/N12 full native and N16/N24 native lower-half.
All are fresh solves from rest over their complete branch-specific new endpoint.
Existing hash-bound meshes may be reused; no new mesher call is needed. Any
representation change needs its own declared and reviewed equivalence evidence.

Required comparisons on that stronger option:

  * Compression S60: all 61 states and 75 fixed physical probes; record N24/32/36
    reaction/motion/trend metrics. Retain the four signed unequal-ratio triplets
    N8/12/16, N12/16/24, N16/24/32, N24/32/36 at every nonrest state. Require
    adjacent reaction and motion absolute limits; latest endpoint admissibility;
    zero unresolved nonrest states, two-latest-limit envelope exceedances,
    increasing-order-drift flags or force-limit-instability flags. Use the
    existing drift comparison (+1e-6), positive-root logic and fixed original
    allowance formula, not thresholds chosen from the new curves. Preserve
    unresolved older triplets and raw trend outcomes rather than concealing them.
  * Compression S120: compare i to 2*i for i=0..60. Require original temporal
    force/probe gates. Replace only N36 with those even S120 states, recompute
    all conditional state classifications and N24/32/36 adjacent classifications,
    and require zero changes, unresolved states, instability or exceedances.
    Recompute and report the endpoint decision sensitivity for the new S60
    baseline; report the actual signed shift. The old sensitivity number is not
    a new-domain acceptance constant.
  * Tension S60: both co-primary triplets N12/16/24 and N8/16/24, including
    reaction/motion magnitude and trend checks, must pass. Tension S120 must
    pass the original temporal force/probe gates; substituting only N24 even
    S120 states into both triplets must leave every classification unchanged.
  * Individual all-frame convergence, residual, sampled positive Jacobians,
    prescribed motion, force/moment balance, work-energy, primitive consistency
    and reconstruction checks apply before any curve participates in a group.

This stronger option still yields conditional new-domain branch qualification,
not rigorous continuum accuracy. Original spatial_acceptance remains false and
all old failures remain immutable. If original formal global convergence is the
required claim, this is not enough; a separately designed homogeneous convergence
study is needed. No outcome of either option releases patient or clinical claims.

3. B2 -- Endpoint/step propagation must cover all primitive checks

The proposal identifies checked_reference() as hard-coded to +/-15%, but the
dependency extends further. mechanics_hbe_branch_calibration_v3_readout.py
stream_frames() generates TIMES*sign*.15*H; common temporal readers do the same.
mechanics_hbe_physics.py read_frame() obtains prescribed displacements from the
old loading model. The full reconstruction checks, work integration, fitted
readout and parsers also assume 121 states or the old schedule. Changing only a
deck, load_coordinate array or reference validator would be insufficient.

Freeze a single explicit branch endpoint and step-grid contract. Pass it through
deck emission, loading receipts, exact schedule/deck verification, half-to-full
reconstruction, full prescribed-boundary checks, work integration, primitive
readout, group comparisons and fitted confirmations. Keep fixed geometry and
probe sites. Do not mutate global H/TIMES/.15 constants or bypass a physics gate.
Pin exact file-kind/mesh/state-count parser caps for N8/N12/N16/N24/N32/N36 as
applicable; N36 logs cannot be passed through the old generic 256 MiB cap.

Specify max|fine force| over the 61 compared even S120 states, matching the
original formula; do not silently use all 121 states as a different denominator.
Require shape (61,75,3) for common probe arrays and Euclidean probe-vector maxima.
For pairwise spatial diagnostics, implement the declared two-level formula
directly rather than inventing a third mesh. Three-level trend gates require
three genuinely distinct new-domain meshes.

Before native execution, verify endpoint literal/float64/XML round trips and
half/full displacement factors. Specify whether strict support is reconstructed
from the prescribed input coordinate or from native printed displacement; they
are different quantities. Under the proposal's inward-rounding-fails rule, the
coverage checker must really consume the independently established interval and
must reject an inward endpoint. It must not merely regenerate the desired array
and label it solved support. Retain the original boundary-error numerical gate;
do not turn it into permission to extrapolate measured coordinates.

Required negative controls include an extended loading receipt paired with old
boundary fields, old +/-15% work coordinates, missing/duplicate/nonmonotone rows,
wrong signs or units, mismatched S60/S120 grids, inward endpoint rounding, source
or primitive drift, attempted fit after failed qualification, and attempted
torque access before durable freeze. A passing scalar coverage test alone does
not test the full new-domain readout path.

4. B3 -- Freeze resource, stage and no-retry contracts before execution

The six-call cap arithmetic is 1800 + 2*2400 + 2*600 + 420 = 8220 s. An 8400 s
inclusive aggregate leaves 180 s if every call reaches its cap. That is not a
logical contradiction: nested limits may be truncated by remaining aggregate
time. It is not a promise that six near-cap solves plus all overhead can finish.
Prior N36/S120 alone used about 180 s beyond native execution through publication,
and its separate independent audit used about 165 s. Declare which reviews run
inside the phase and bound any later independent review separately.

The six exact old-domain analogues sum to 3672.4775739158504 native seconds and
3070506998 retained run-directory bytes. The six additional runs sum to
268.06708575203083 seconds and 446866363 bytes. All twelve sum to
3940.544659667881 seconds and 3517373361 bytes. These are historical observations
at old endpoints, not forecasts or guaranteed upper bounds at the new endpoints.
Run-directory totals exclude source archives, preparation, audit and closeout.
The full per-run execution hashes and stat-only totals are in resource-audit.json.

For a 12-run planning option, retaining proposed caps and adding four N8/N12 calls
at 90 s, N16 at 420 s and N24 at 600 s gives 9600 s summed native maxima. An
inclusive 10800 s ceiling would reserve 1200 s at those maxima, but it is only
an unapproved planning example and requires real accounting before freezing.
Do not quietly reuse the six-call 8400 s contract or increase it after failure.
The 6 GiB cap is plausible compared with the 3.52 GB historical raw-run total,
but must charge every new archive, deck, receipt, parser spill, log and partial
output on the same filesystem. Keep old raw data as bound inputs instead of
copying it. Recheck free space immediately before an actual release.

Retain one runtime thread, 3 GiB sampled whole-process-family RSS, zero meshing
and streaming readout. Historical largest N36 sampled RSS is about 1.92 GB; this
supports plausibility, not a continuous-peak guarantee. Define active per-run
output/parser caps by level/steps and total new-output accounting. All deadlines
must be min(stage/call cap, remaining inclusive aggregate), beginning before
preflight/hashing and ending only at durable publication/cleanup.

Freeze exact order, attempt marker, per-run IDs, source/archive/runtime/mesh hashes
and phase transitions. Count a native invocation before spawn and charge failed
or partial calls. Stop on the first mandatory failure, preserve partial outputs,
kill and reap the process family; no resume, automatic retry, alternate endpoint,
extra mesh, cap reset or new directory under the same version to evade the stop.
A later proposal gets a new version with the entire prior failed history bound.
Reporting-only audit repairs must preserve both attempts and cannot repeat a solve.

Six diagnostic references plus two fitted solves are at most eight new native
calls only if a separately justified qualification contract exists; twelve
stronger references plus two fitted solves are fourteen. Do not charge the
fitted phase to the reference-call cap or use a second release to reset a failed
reference phase. A fitted release must bind the exact successful independent
qualification receipt, endpoint/vector hashes, accepted references and complete
phase history. It must fix its own inclusive time/output caps before execution.

5. Held-out seal, fit and interpretation

Preserving N12/S120 signed torsion prediction references is defensible because
their torsion load path is unchanged. Existing numerical torsion references are
simulation outputs, distinct from measured torque members. The conditional
homogeneity proof supports scaling on the same fixed equilibrium branch, not
uniqueness, a numerical guarantee at arbitrary fitted modulus, or measured
accuracy. The two actual fitted axial confirmations remain mandatory on the new
N36/S120 and N24/S120 reference schedules, with original component-wise motion,
raw-reaction and torque scaling tolerances, actual-mu physics scales and complete
primitive/source bindings. Reject nonpositive or unidentified scales without
clipping, refitting or another native attempt.

Only after both confirmations and independent paired primitive checks may a
durable source-bound parameter-and-prediction freeze precede measured torsion
access. Preserve the exact two torsion member identities and the role split.
Their headers and coordinates remain uninspected here. A later strict parser or
range failure after the first reveal is a terminal recorded failure; the mode
has then been exposed and cannot become unseen validation again. Freeze metrics,
normalization and null-denominator rules before reveal, without an empirical pass
threshold. A fit is within-specimen calibration goodness, and torsion is a
same-specimen different-mode prediction, not donor transfer or patient-specific
stiffness, surgical retraction/cutting/injury or neurological validation.
