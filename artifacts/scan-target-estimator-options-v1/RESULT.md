# Preoperative scan-derived nominal target: bounded option review

Reviewed 2026-10-09 from source manifests and primary publications only. No patient array, model weight or patient inference was opened or run. This is an option for a separate inference-only track, not an admitted tumor target or clinical result.

**Candidate.** Test [GlioMODA source commit `4846707da3fe41255620d6dc6f4a71991cb3b1e1`](https://github.com/BrainLesion/GlioMODA/commit/4846707da3fe41255620d6dc6f4a71991cb3b1e1) `t1c+t2f` mode on protected RESECT Case4 **DEVELOPMENT**, but only after verifying Case4-specific contrast/FLAIR identities, within-patient correspondence and the model's pinned T1c-reference registration, skull stripping and SRI-24 normalization, source/target affines, and inverse-transform round trip into the native FLAIR frame. Fail closed on a missing or unqualified step. The [creator's RESECT-SEG methods](https://aapm.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/mp.17317) describe contrast-enhanced T1 plus T2-FLAIR preoperative MRI and a separate preoperative MRI tumor reference; the pinned local Case4 manifest names `T1` and `FLAIR`, but its filenames alone do not verify contrast or annotation alignment. [GlioMODA's pinned source mode table](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/BrainLesion/GlioMODA/4846707da3fe41255620d6dc6f4a71991cb3b1e1/gliomoda/constants.py) explicitly includes that pair. Its [BraTS 2021 study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12989096/) favors the pair on curated preoperative data, predominantly high-grade glioma; transfer to RESECT's low-grade cases is unknown. Whole-tumor/FLAIR abnormality is a visible imaging estimate, not tissue prescribed for removal, neurological risk, or a calibrated probability.

**Fixed weights, rights and alternatives.** Bind the source commit above to [GlioMODA weight record v1.0.2](https://zenodo.org/records/15625233): `weights.zip`, **3,306,003,582 bytes**, published MD5 `428d96a19480ecfa391b3cdd5a7a535b` ([record metadata](https://zenodo.org/api/records/15625233), CC-BY-4.0). On an authorized later acquisition, verify those fields, compute and pin SHA-256, verify extracted file hashes, and use an immutable local folder. **Do not instantiate the unmodified `Inferer`/`ModelHandler` or call `check_weights_path` in a run:** [pinned `weights.py`](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/BrainLesion/GlioMODA/4846707da3fe41255620d6dc6f4a71991cb3b1e1/gliomoda/weights.py) follows a mutable latest-record URL, can delete a local weight folder on version mismatch, and buffers the 3.3 GB archive in memory. A future adapter must prevent network/version mutation and abort if the exact pinned local weights are absent. No archive was downloaded here. The GlioMODA code repository exposes no detected code license or `LICENSE` file, so weight rights do not establish permission to embed its code in a company app. The paper's CC-BY-NC license is for the publication. The local RESECT-SEG rights record is CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0; keep this pilot research-only and clarify rights before commercial use. The [MONAI BraTS bundle](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Project-MONAI/model-zoo/dev/models/brats_mri_segmentation/docs/README.md) has an [Apache-2.0 bundle license](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Project-MONAI/model-zoo/dev/models/brats_mri_segmentation/LICENSE), but requires all four registered sequences and cannot be run on the current Case4 pair by filling channels. BTC's structural minimum has native T1w plus source fractional mask, without FLAIR or contrast. GlioMODA lists a T1-native-only mode, yet its study reports weaker reduced-input behavior; test it only as a separately labeled failure/abstention condition.

**Evaluation boundary.** Preserve `manifests/resect-component-cohort-v1.json`: 14 TRAIN people, four SELECT, four MEASUREMENT_EVAL and Case4 DEVELOPMENT, with all visits grouped. Freeze preprocessing, weights, mode, exact WT label conversion, empty-mask handling, boundary-metric definitions and abstention before consulting SELECT; use SELECT for the declared choice and keep MEASUREMENT_EVAL sealed until that choice is fixed. For this pilot, map **only GlioMODA whole-tumor (WT)** to the independently reviewed **preoperative FLAIR MRI tumor reference**, after checking that their label definitions and native physical frames permit comparison. Report Dice, HD95, signed volume and boundary error for that mapping, plus failures/coverage; **do not score enhancing tumor or tumor core against the FLAIR label**. Hidden references may not enter inference or route proposals. The four held-out RESECT people provide same-cohort measurement evidence, **not** an outer FINAL result. `manifests/cohort_registry.json` identifies UCSF-PDGM-0004 as BraTS2021_00097, so this known overlap disqualifies it as an independent GlioMODA test. Preserve UPenn-GBM as an unopened outer-final candidate pending BraTS/UPenn identity-overlap and modality QC. If inputs, alignment, label meaning or rights cannot be established, use an explicitly annotation-assisted surgeon-reviewable mask or abstain in inference-only mode. The geometric planner remains the baseline; no patient estimator or route improvement has been demonstrated.
