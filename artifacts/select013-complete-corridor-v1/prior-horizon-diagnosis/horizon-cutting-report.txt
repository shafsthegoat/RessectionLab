Conclusion
The saved records do not establish that a fresh 48-action route to any selected straight target corridor is impossible. They establish an expensive widening mechanism and poor prior allocation of actions. Do not increase horizon on this evidence. The forthcoming fresh persistent-corridor run is still a meaningful h48 test; this note does not delay it.

What the source actually does
native_resection.py:271 contained_capsule_cells requires all eight affine cell corners inside the active capsule, using radius minus1e-10mm. Partial touches receive no removal credit. At lines565–576 preview_stroke sweeps the active tip from previous_tip minus tip_length to current_tip; the shaft is tested against the PRIOR temporary cavity before any cells first cut in that microstep receive credit. If the shaft hits remaining tissue the whole proposed stroke is rejected; any prior within-preview removals are uncommitted. At lines627–638, only full-cell candidate components exposed by face adjacency to connected free space are removed, and those removals extend temporary free space for later microsteps. Thus the algorithm can cut several cells in one microstep or in several successive microsteps of a single action; there is no one-cell/action clipping.

The source defaults to at most 0.25mm tip spacing (native_resection.py:105). Generic suction has1.0mm active radius,1.4mm shaft radius,3mm active centerline and100mm working length. Aspirator has1.7/2.7/4/90mm respectively (geometry.py:109). These working lengths exceed the18–19mm target approach, so nominal tool reach alone is not the depth cap. A larger shaft than tip creates a real modeled preparation requirement; extending the endpoint does not let the tip borrow future side cuts to clear that shaft.

Whole-cell transverse constraint, in this recorded near-unit source-axis geometry
For a cell centered on a normal source-grid axis, the maximum transverse corner distance is sqrt(.5^2+.5^2)≈0.7071mm. For a neighboring transverse column it is sqrt(1.5^2+.5^2)≈1.5811mm. The1.0mm suction tip can therefore fully contain only its own integer-centered transverse column (subject to axial caps/exposure), while the1.4mm shaft intersects adjacent side and diagonal columns: their nearest transverse box distances are0.5 and≈0.7071mm. A3×3 neighborhood is consequently a useful dense-tissue illustration of shaft width, but its actual occupancy is NOT known from the saved first-failure sets. Aspirator can encompass the four neighboring columns at1.5811mm but not diagonal corner2.1213mm; it also brings a much wider shaft. These are geometry inequalities with large margins, not full native feasibility claims.

The public6mm aperture permits approximately4.6mm transverse suction axis offset and3.3mm aspirator offset for these normal axes. This explains why some useful wider-tip actions can be unavailable even when a suction axis fits. It does not prove every relevant aspirator corridor is impossible; actual shaft, domain and exposure tests still govern.

Why many successful actions stop after one cell
native_proposals.py:_tool_footprint_endpoint scans positive-depth endpoint centers in order and returns the FIRST endpoint containing any exposed full cell (lines841–851). It intentionally does not optimize volume or guarantee shaft clearance. Residual obstruction proposals end at a current blocker cell center. Consequently a shallow one-cell endpoint is often a proposal property. A target endpoint may permit a longer cut once its shaft corridor is prepared, but that is untested at this state. Treating one observed cell/action as a physics upper bound, multiplying depth by that rate, or dividing desired volume by it would be invalid.

A narrow lower bound that IS supported
At the saved47 state, proximal nominal suction ray7 (endpoint[20,89,85],18.5mm) first fails on[5,88,84] and[5,88,86]. Both lie diagonally off its axis and cannot be fully contained by that ray's1mm tip. Every currently legal original/residual action removes only one cell, including the independently verified complementary prep branches. Therefore, under that saved current catalog, reaching that exact ray requires at least two preparatory non-STOP actions plus the ray itself. It cannot fit as a continuation of the already spent47 actions under h48. This is not a lower bound for a fresh route, the new v3 catalog, or any other target ray: a fresh route can avoid irrelevant earlier cuts, and future proposals/aspirator cuts may cover several cells.

What cannot be inferred without new public execution
The first-failure sidecars censor later corridor occupancy. Saved touched cells and the47 cuts do not supply all occupied cells along18–19mm approaches. We also lack a proven maximum cells removable per future feasible action, a minimal required cut set per corridor, and a proof that larger-tip actions stay inaccessible after preparation. Hence no rigorous lower bound exceeding48 is derivable here. The tempting8 lateral cells×unknown remaining axial layers calculation assumes dense occupancy and one-cell cuts and must not be presented as a bound.

Diagnostic fields to interpret the already planned complete route
For each committed step, retain chosen exact goal geometry, current complete/censored first-failure set, its physically removed subset, newly revealed co-blockers, actual removed cells by tool and depth, remaining horizon, cumulative reward, and reason for abandoning/stopping a corridor. If the route spends48 actions monotonically clearing one corridor while consistently revealing deeper blockers, that supports horizon/cost pressure for that policy. If it repeats sideways near-depth cuts or switches corridors, that supports allocation/dependency failure. If no emitted legal action removes the current mandatory set, that supports candidate/geometry failure. None proves all possible sequences impossible, and positive target reach alone does not establish positive complete return or clinical safety.
