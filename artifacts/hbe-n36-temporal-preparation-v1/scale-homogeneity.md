# Conditional scale homogeneity of the frozen specimen model

Analytical reuse across meshes is justified **for the same equilibrium branch of each fixed discretization**. It is not an empirical scale check on N36 or a release to calibrate. Live S120 outputs and measured responses were not opened; no solver, mesh generation, raw-output replay or fit was performed.

The actual deck sets Ogden c1=2μ, exponent 2, other coefficients zero, K=(149/3)μ, pressure model 1, three-field hex8 and augmentation off. The pinned FEBio sources and independent reconstruction give

`Wμ(F) = μ[(Ibar1−3)/2 + (149/12)(J²−1−2 ln J)]`.

For the actual three-field discretization the volumetric term uses cell-average J. On any fixed mesh, quadrature, geometry and displacement boundary path, its discrete energy is `Eμ(u)=μ E1(u)`. There are no body forces, prescribed nonzero tractions, contact, inertia or prestress introducing a separate force scale. Thus multiplying positive μ by a leaves the stationary displacement set and local stability signs unchanged; selecting the same branch gives identical u/J and scales stress, pressure, all nodal reactions, force, torque and energy by a. Current lever arms are unchanged. This applies separately to full-height and half-height meshes with their fixed constraints; half-to-full force factor 1 and energy factor 2 remain fixed. It does not equate different meshes' displacement fields or prove the symmetry assumption.

For exact Newton arithmetic both tangent and residual scale by a, leaving the displacement increment unchanged. The inspected solver uses squared residual norms (scale a²), displacement norms (unchanged) and energy norms (scale a); its relative tests and the explicitly prescribed `(10⁻¹⁰ μR²)²` residual floor transform consistently. This removes a known absolute-tolerance inconsistency, but does not certify every BFGS, line-search, linear-factorization, rounding or output-precision decision. Finite tolerances, near-singular/bifurcating states, conditioning and extreme fitted μ can still change numerical trajectories. Positive scaling preserves the equilibrium set, not uniqueness or the algorithm's selected branch.

The saved original N12/S120 controls (121 states, μ=1000→2000 Pa) pass. Compression motion error is 1.0000247e−14 m versus 1.1337385e−8 m; its reaction/torque tolerance ratios are 6.0084282e−8 and 1.2450867e−6. Positive torsion motion error is 1.0000030e−14 m versus 1.1321478e−8 m; ratios are 4.6344746e−8 and 4.2322088e−9. These are hash-bound saved results, not independently replayed scale primitives here. Original mesh failures and both unexecuted fitted runs remain failures/missing evidence; neither these controls nor the algebra repairs them.

If numerical gates later permit compression N36, tension N24 and torsion N12, freeze that branch/mesh/schedule/reference/backend map before measurement access. Existing calibration functions hardcode N12/S120 and cannot be silently relabeled. One common positive μ scale may be fitted from the two axial reference predictions, with original weighting and no offset/shape/pressure adjustment. The algebra permits scaling a separately accepted torsion reference; it supplies no held-out physical accuracy.

Two actual fitted confirmations are still required: compression and tension, each on its own frozen selected mesh and schedule at μ̂, compared with that same mesh's reference run. Require exact deck identity except c1, K and the squared residual floor; actual completed state count and resource/source bindings; unchanged nodal displacement, scaled raw nodal reactions and torque at every state; independently reconstructed positive Jacobians, boundary balance, prescribed motion and work/energy consistency. Preserve original scale tolerances: motion `10⁻⁶R+10⁻⁵ max|u_ref|`; each reaction `10⁻⁷ μ̂R²+10⁻⁴|a R_ref|`; torque `10⁻⁷ μ̂R³+10⁻⁴|a T_ref|`. Readout scales must use actual μ̂. Reject any incomplete/failed confirmation without relaxing thresholds or refitting; freeze predictions only after both pass. No empirical N24/N36 scaling, calibration, temporal acceptance or physical validation is claimed by this note.
