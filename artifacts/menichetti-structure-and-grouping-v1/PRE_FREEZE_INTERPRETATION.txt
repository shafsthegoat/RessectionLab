Menichetti saved structural result and proposed brain-group roles

The saved report is a complete supported structural inventory, not a parser
refusal: 131 nodes comprise 11 scalar structs and 120 double matrices. One root
F_t_curves struct contains Brain1 through Brain10. Each brain contains the same
12 region fields. Every leaf is directly a 10,350-row numeric matrix; no cells,
character arrays, timing arrays, motion arrays, or further metadata fields occur.
No original MAT was reopened for this analysis. Numeric/character array values,
validity, padding, contact and measured position remain unexamined.

Creator metadata supports the interpretation of each column as one force test;
it declares force in micronewtons at 10 kHz. Thus nominal conversion is 1e-6 N/µN
and sample spacing 0.0001 s. These come from the public creator description, not
from reading leaf values. All shapes together contain 595 source-described trial
columns. They are dependent observations from 10 source-declared brain groups,
not 595 independent donors.

  Exact inclusive MATLAB one-based column indices are recorded per region in

Proposed roles, not frozen and not a response-access release
  Training: Brain1, Brain4, Brain5, Brain7, Brain8, Brain10 (357 columns).
  Development: Brain2, Brain9 (119 columns).
  Sealed test: Brain3, Brain6 (119 columns).
  Assignment sorts SHA256(salt + newline + original response SHA256 + newline
  + exact brain alias), with fixed salt menichetti-brain-group-split-v1, and takes
  the first 6 / next 2 / last 2 groups. No response, demographic, or trial-count balancing
  enters the selection. This is a source-declared brain-specimen split. The
  inventory does not independently authenticate donor identities across aliases.
  HBE, Greiner and patient splits remain unchanged.

Timing consequence and next supported step
  The complete schema has no timing or displacement fields. The uniform 10,350
  rows are consistent with about 350 nominal ramp samples plus 10,000 hold samples,
  but that arithmetic does not establish actual contact, ramp onset, hold onset,
  contiguous sampling or measured indentation. Do not impose sample 351 as a
  certified hold boundary. Published motor depth/speed remain nominal commands.
  After root freezes groups and separately reviews a measured-access protocol,
  training-only validity checks can proceed. An independent relaxation-law test
  still needs a defensible hold-alignment rule declared before test access. With
  no independent alignment evidence, the accessible narrower test is fixed-prefix
  prediction of future sampled force; it must carry that limited claim. Freeze
  prefix/horizon, preprocessing, exclusions, nuisance handling, models/baselines
  and scoring before exposing test responses. Do not optimize alignment on test
  suffixes. Aggregate by brain and region, acknowledging only 2 test brain groups.
  No fitting, measured-response interpretation, or physical validation occurred.

Population scope remains 10 postmortem human brains aged 64–94, PBS/4°C storage,
about 4±1 days postmortem, tested at about 22°C without preconditioning. Destructive
sites within regions and regions within brains remain grouped. This is not
living surgical tissue evidence. StatisticsTables stays excluded.
