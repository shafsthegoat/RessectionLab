# Open neurosurgical interaction measurements: read-only access audit

Checked 2026-10-09 UTC. Scope: public primary papers, creator repositories, and official dataset pages; no bulk or scientific payload downloaded, no tracked file edited, no donor/patient role changed. This is a bounded search, **not** proof that no other data exist. “Public paper” and “public simulator code” do not imply reusable measured trial data. The question was whether an accessible record pairs a known brain-directed instrument motion/load with independent tissue displacement or measured force, at trial level, so that a narrow action-response model could be tested without treating its own simulator output as truth.

## Finding

I found **no verified, openly downloadable, same-trial brain retraction/manipulation record** that provides the full set of instrument pose/contact trajectory, force or torque, independent tissue displacement, spatial registration, and usable rights. Three complementary evidence streams exist, and none closes that joint gate:

Do not combine it with RESECT or another cohort as if recorded on the same patients/times. Force distributions or surgeon-skill cutoffs are not tissue injury probabilities. |
| [Binhammad et al., arXiv 2609.14198](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.14198v1) | Fresh ex-vivo calf brain in printed skull with calibrated sub-skull six-axis sensor; robot imposed 2.5-mm downward motion and measured tool/skull loads. The article's own limitation says sensor measures **net transmitted** load, without contact localization/multi-contact resolution. I found no linked raw release/data license or independent tissue-displacement field on its official arXiv record. | Useful **force metrology precedent**; not an independently executable brain tissue-response validation set, and not a live patient. |

Additional [BITE creator images](https://nist.mni.mcgill.ca/bite-brain-images-of-tumors-for-evaluation-database/) and [ReMIND TCIA collection](https://www.cancerimagingarchive.net/collection/remind/) add intraoperative imaging, not synchronized interaction forces. A [stereo force/deformation study on an elastomer brain replica](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4893380/) reports a sensorized tracked dissector and deformation measurements in the paper; no verified public raw paired release/rights was found. These can motivate an acquisition protocol, but published curves and figures do not permit independent raw-trial validation. A simulator's “ground truth forces,” including in the MICCAI SOFA work, are **generated labels**, not measured physical response.

## Strongest credible next validation route

1. Keep the existing geometric planner as the baseline and **do not open a surgical action-response reward gate**. Finish HBE's independent numerical convergence and its predeclared measured held-out mode (including force/torque and fixture geometry) to establish only a specimen material-model result. Keep the failed/refinement results visible. This is the one already acquired brain-specific force source with verified provider fixity, but it is not a tool-interaction benchmark.
2. If a secure, source-authenticated run005 route becomes available without changing the accepted donor/run, first inspect only schema, timestamps, native units, six-axis tip/femur transforms and a minimal VIC-3D pairing. Freeze contact geometry, synchronized trial exclusions and donor-grouped roles **before** fitting. Then use an established FEM/contact framework (FEBio or SOFA, independently numerically verified) to predict withheld **human-leg indentation** load and surface motion. If HTTPS/fixity or camera/tool registration cannot be established, keep the benchmark blocked; do not swap in run027 or trust HTTP SHA as independent provenance. This tests interaction machinery only, not brain surgery.
3. For real anatomy, select an untouched RESECT patient and separately validate **conditional displacement** with same-information rigid/IDW and FEM comparisons after anatomy, frame, mesh and observation gates pass. Report numerical convergence and held-out landmark errors. A successful image-updating result cannot be promoted to retractor-force or cutting fidelity.
4. The direct brain action-response gate requires a lawful new or newly accessible **paired** physical record: pre-action geometry, calibrated tracked tool/contact history, synchronized six-axis load at the relevant interface, independent registered tissue motion (e.g., stereo/US/CT beads), known fixture/support and timing, metrology/uncertainty, rights and patient/specimen split. The phantom/animal and 2026 calf-brain papers show feasible acquisition designs, but no verified raw record found in this audit fills that role. A physical phantom can validate engineering displacement/contact implementation; ex-vivo brain can test biological force transfer; neither alone establishes patient safety or clinical injury thresholds.

**Decision:** currently no defensible claim that simulated controlled brain retraction predicts measured tool response in a new patient. The physical-measurement dependency is open. Separately labeled simulator training may proceed for a geometric or simulator-defined task, with same-test-information search/IL/RL comparisons and held-out real patient transfer checks, but those comparisons must not cite simulator rewards as clinical tissue injury evidence.
