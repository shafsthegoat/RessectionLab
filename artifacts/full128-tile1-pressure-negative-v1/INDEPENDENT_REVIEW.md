The previous 64³ tile1-versus-tile4 parity and sampled RSS reduction do not extrapolate to full128. Both observed full128 tiled attempts ended under host-pressure guards. A different-host, one-run peak comparison between tile depths is not evidence that one operator setting is better or worse at full128.
