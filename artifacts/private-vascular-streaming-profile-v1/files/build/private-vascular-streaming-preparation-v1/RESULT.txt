Generated tiled vascular contact feasibility candidate — preparation only

Concrete problem: the integrated evaluator's contact helper materializes the capsule's entire voxel bounding box and keeps global Python sets of contacts. A long oblique sweep through a normal-sized image can therefore allocate large coordinate/AABB arrays even though only a thin tube intersects the tool. Raising that evaluator's small-grid caps alone would hide this cost.

This ignored successor streams disjoint fixed tiles. The existing independent segment/box oracle conservatively admits tiles, then evaluates their cells in bounded 256-cell batches. Shaft, tip, per-action and whole-strategy masks are unioned within each tile before counts accumulate. Repeated strokes/actions cannot multiply whole-strategy contact counts. Each tile is discarded before the next tile. No whole-grid contact set, label volume, coordinate array, image reader or learned model is created.

The full reference field of view is traversed through bounded tile descriptors; nominal tissue support never crops the query. Grid frames are explicit orthogonal RAS affines; shears refuse. Capsule endpoints are already in reference RAS. Rotation, anisotropy, voxel-centre and stop-exclusive tile-boundary conventions are tested against the existing scalar oracle. Final cell contacts retain the exact 1e-10 mm² squared-distance tolerance. Broad-phase tile boxes alone have a recorded 1e-9*coordinate-scale-mm padding, making tile admission more inclusive without changing final contact decisions. This is a numerical implementation check, not a formal floating-point or clinical geometry proof.

The provider is an exact generated procedural-reference class, with fixed lattice/empty/all-covered masks and explicit full/half/none coverage. It accepts no private reader callback. Positive labels are restricted to declared coverage. Missing annotation coverage and outside-FOV motion preserve unknown outcomes. The source identity describes that procedural control, not a source-authenticated patient volume. Existing real-person, registration, QC, lineage, sealed-history and admission gates remain closed and unchanged.

This is a contact kernel, not a replacement strategy evaluator: it neither loads/replays complete plans nor verifies tool kinematics. A future adapter must derive capsules from every independently checked microstep after the complete-strategy seal. Removed-cell overlap is explicitly not evaluated here; the integrated evaluator's congruence requirement and separate removal metric are unchanged. No target truth, injury probability, continuous intersection volume or vessel-free biological claim is introduced.

Bounds: at most128 capsules, at most16³ cells per tile, at most256 tile descriptors per coarse batch, and explicit budgets for charged tile/capsule pairs, reserved cell/capsule pairs, sampled cells and cooperative wall time. Budget refusal exposes work counters only, with no partial outcome marked complete. Counters for reserved work may exceed work actually performed if a later cancellation occurs. No hard wall/RSS containment is claimed; substantial profiling requires a separately serialized root-owned run.
