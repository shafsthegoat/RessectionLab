# ReMIND full training-component intake preparation

The frozen [cohort declaration](../../manifests/experiments/remind-component-cohort-v1.json) preserves ReMIND001 as DEVELOPMENT and assigns the other 113 people by a deterministic, namespaced ID hash: **79 TRAIN, 17 SELECT, 17 MEASUREMENT_EVAL**. Every timepoint and linked derivative inherits the person role. No image, outcome, quality or performance was used to choose the new split; prior metadata exposure is retained in the declaration.

The first payload scope is all indexed raw MRI/ultrasound series from the 79 TRAIN people: **692 series, 59,520 objects, 30,173,493,690 exact bytes**. Exact HTTPS object listings now confirm the earlier rounded index estimate. There are 59,297 objects with source single-part MD5 and 223 ultrasound objects with official GCS generation-pinned whole-object CRC32C. Segmentation derivatives are excluded from this first raw-imaging scope and from observed-image counts. No new patient payload was read before role freeze. The transfer runner must bind the new committed declaration hash before downloading.

This is a surgical anatomy/imaging component cohort, not an all-glioma cohort. The historical strict three-label diagnosis predicate matches 88 people overall, including 63 TRAIN. The other stratum includes labels such as Low grade glioma and must not be called entirely non-glioma. Neither stratum is admitted for policy training. Source diagnosis metadata is only cohort-eligibility evidence; it is not a permitted preoperative policy input. The exact predicate, restricted workbook fields and source hash remain explicit.

Roles are reserved before acquisition; QC failure will not cause replacement from SELECT or MEASUREMENT_EVAL. Existing cohort assignments remain unchanged. Cross-source identity and pretrained exposure remain unresolved where not proven, so no independent-transfer claim follows. Intraoperative images are not preoperative observations or tool-action/force recordings. All downloaded bytes remain unreviewed until their intended-use checks.
