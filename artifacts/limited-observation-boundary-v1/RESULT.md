# Limited-observation boundary: generated-fixture vertical slice

The committed boundary at `a0c403a` accepts one exact generated spatial fixture. It builds nominal planning state from a limited-input specification, seals a complete action sequence, and only then calls the evaluator-owned private target loader. A fresh independent replay scores that fixed sequence against the hidden target. This is a software boundary for a target-only counterfactual, **not** patient admission, held-out patient transfer, physical surgery, or file/process isolation.

The owner passed 60 focused controls. An independent reviewer passed the same 60 plus 131 additional controls and issued a scope-limited GO. In the positive control, the identical two-action sealed plan, geometry, proposals, legal masks, cavity, nominal observations, source hash, and planning accounting produced 2.0 versus 0.0 mm³ private target removed and rewards 1.1 versus −1.3 after swapping the private reference. The loader ran after full nominal replay. This proves the private target changes independent scoring without changing that fixture's plan; it does not prove hidden support or vascular hazards are excluded from a future patient task.

The wider related suite had 126 passes and 10 failures. The independent reviewer reproduced all 10 failures on frozen base commit `e596c8f` with the new module absent: the legacy provisional-support loader rejects unverified weight lineage. The failures remain open and cannot be counted as new boundary regressions or bypassed by this slice.
