Post-exposure actor representation: source and saved-metadata finding

Established input visibility
The actor receives a fixed native-grid crop, not a whole-volume 64³ resize.
NativeSpatialCase places it from the access, offsets its center inward by one
quarter of its axis length, clips it to the source, and keeps it fixed throughout
the episode. Array slicing preserves native samples; only intensity normalization
is applied. All four crops are 64³ at approximately 0.9765625/0.9765625/1 mm spacing,
or 62.5×62.5×64 mm cell extent. Affine reconciliation preserves sample indices.

Saved source-affine/crop/history arithmetic finds all 109 removed cells inside the
actor crops: 58 on 015 and 51 on 045, over 25 motions. Seven motions remove exactly
one cell. A newly removed occupied cell becomes connected-free/cavity 1 within
interaction-domain coverage, so these changes do not vanish at the DTO stage.
002/018 have no committed motions. Every initially emitted ray has 5/5 samples
inside the crop. Positive teacher entry-to-tip lengths are 0.488 mm on 015 and
1.465–3.418 mm on 045: the five centerline samples are 0.122 mm and 0.366–0.854 mm apart.
These short teacher rays provide no evidence of coarse axial sampling failure.

Scale and compression
Tools have active radii 1.0/1.7 mm, shaft radii 1.4/2.7 mm, and active centerline
lengths 3/4 mm. One voxel is about 0.954 mm³. Adjacent source-voxel endpoints differ
by 2/63≈0.03175 in normalized action coordinates; they are not quantized together.

The encoder has two stride-one 3³ convolutions (18→8→16 channels;5³ receptive
field), followed by 2³ adaptive-average pooling. Each global bin averages 32768
encoded sites over about 31.25×31.25×32 mm. One input-cell change can affect at most
125 encoded sites/channel before pooling. This is a support bound, not a measured
activation or logit attenuation. Each action additionally samples five local
encoded centerline features with trilinear interpolation and in-bounds flags.

The 16 action scalars carry poses, direction, tool dimensions, angle and tool
change; prospective removed-cell count, target gain and reward are absent.
Nominal target/tissue remain static; the network must combine them with changing
cavity to infer remaining material. The 16 global target scalars keep full-target
centroid/bounds/mass static; only committed-overlap fraction changes. One target
cell contributes 0.0001291 on 015 and 0.00003952 on 045. These increments are not
float 32-zero. The actor also gets centroid-to-tip relation 4. STOP uses pooled
context/state/spacing/global target; only the critic additionally receives the
mean/max/count summary of legal candidate geometry and ray features.

What is not established
No weights, real observation arrays or model activations were read. Actual actor
feature aliasing and a causal learned-compression failure are not demonstrated.
Out-of-crop arrangements with equal overlap and identical remaining model inputs
would be indistinguishable, but this conditional invariance is not evidence that
the observed 109 inside-crop removals are invisible. Coarse pooling, static target
summaries and learned target/cavity combination are plausible limitations only.
Earlier annotation-to-MR nearest-neighbor sampling is a separate lossy stage:
saved 002 tumor counts 24294→24274;015/018/045 counts unchanged. This does not arise
from the actor crop and does not identify particular lost cells.

One conditional diagnostic, after fixed endpoints
If failures remain, use one frozen 29-state TRAIN teacher feature/readout audit:
measure raw cavity deltas, pooled/ray feature deltas and STOP/matched-physical-
action logits under fixed endpoint weights. Record unmatched actions explicitly.
Exact feature equality could establish lost information; distinguishable features
with wrong ranking would direct diagnosis elsewhere. No optimizer/search/new
labels, architecture sweep, current-run tuning or SELECT-driven choice is implied.

Reproduction and scope
Run derive_saved.py from this directory with stdlib Python. It reads only the
21 files pinned in input-pins.json, verifies the seven inspected source hashes
against the frozen IL 64 source closure, and joins saved episode hashes to its
release. scalar-calculations.json contains crop/spacing/action-count arithmetic;
it contains no image, observation, weights or copied motion/cell-index arrays.
Source anchors: native_spatial_task.py crop 420/channels 550/observation 796;
spatial_policy.py encoder 129/inputs 213/pooling 269; spatial_observations.py 390;
public_target_context.py 229; geometry.py 109. Exact bytes, not line numbers, are
pinned. This is a representation audit of supplied public TRAIN evidence under
an explicit simulated exposure assumption, not anatomy or clinical validation.
